You hold college-wide authority over **General Education** Course assignments.
You can create, edit, reassign, deactivate, and delete them.

## Before you start

- The Course must have **Course Scope = General Education**. A Program-specific
  Course belongs to its Program Head.
- The Faculty member needs a Faculty profile before an assignment can point at them.
- A Program Head may create Program-specific assignments independently; there is no need to coordinate unless the same person teaches both.

## Create an assignment

1. Open **Course Assignments**.
2. Select **Assign Faculty to Course**.
3. Choose the General Education **Course**.
4. Confirm the **Academic Period**, **Year Level**, and **Section** — catalog
   defaults prefill them but do not force them.
5. Choose the **Faculty** member and confirm.

## Edit an assignment

Open the assignment to change its period, year level, or section. Changing the
period on a running or completed evaluation does not move that evaluation; it
stays attached to the assignment as published.

## Change the Faculty owner

Edit the assignment and change **Faculty**. The roster, CILOs, alignments, and
published evaluations stay with the assignment. The previous Faculty member
immediately loses access.

Do this before the term starts wherever possible.

## Deactivate or reactivate

Deactivate to retire an assignment without losing its history, and reactivate to
bring it back. Deactivation does not delete submitted responses.

## Delete an assignment

Deletion is blocked while a published evaluation or a submitted response
references the assignment. Deactivate instead.

## Why roster management is separate

:::caution
You assign Faculty; you do not maintain the roster. The assigned Faculty member
maintains it, and rosters belong to whoever is teaching. See
[Course rosters](/faculty/course-rosters/).
:::
