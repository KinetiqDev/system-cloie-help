The **Courses** page lists the college's General Education Courses — the Courses
whose **Course Scope** is General Education.

## What this page shows

- **Course Code** and **Course Title**.
- The Course's catalog defaults — default semester, term, and year level.
- Its current status and, where shown, which Faculty currently hold assignments.

Use **All Programs** and **All Statuses** to filter; GE Courses belong to no
single program, so the program filter matters less here than it does for
Program-specific Courses.

## What you can change here

- Review the GE catalog as exposed to your role.
- Correct the catalog details and defaults a GE Course shows.

:::caution
The catalog itself — creating Courses, importing CSVs, and deleting them — is
**Secretary** work. You assign Faculty to GE Courses from the
[General Education Course assignments](/gen-ed-coordinator/course-assignments/)
page; that is the authority you actually hold.
:::

## What changes after an assignment

Once Faculty are assigned to a GE Course, they maintain its roster and CILOs, and
map those CILOs to ILOs. See [Course rosters](/faculty/course-rosters/) and
[Align CILOs with outcomes](/faculty/outcome-alignment/).
