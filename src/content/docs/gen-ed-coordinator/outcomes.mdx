**Outcomes** holds the Institutional Learning Outcome catalog. As General
Education Coordinator you are the only role that can change it.

## What an ILO is

An **Institutional Learning Outcome (ILO)** is an outcome the college expects
across every program. There are only a handful, they change rarely, and every
General Education Course supports them rather than a program's Graduate
Outcomes.

:::caution
The Secretary, Dean, Program Head, and Faculty all **read** ILOs. None of them can
create, edit, reorder, archive, or restore one.
:::

## Create an ILO

1. Open **Outcomes**.
2. Select the create action.
3. Enter the **ILO Code** — short, stable, and used by GE mappings.
4. Enter the description.
5. Save.

An ILO code is referenced by General Education mappings. Changing it later
re-keys every mapping that used it, so choose it carefully.

## Edit an ILO

Open the ILO and change its description. Codes are treated as identifiers; if a
code genuinely has to change, confirm the affected GE alignments afterwards.

## Reorder ILOs

**Drag rows to reorder.** The order you set is the order shown everywhere the
catalog is listed — in the GE alignment workspace and in analytics.

## Archive an ILO

Archiving removes an ILO from new mapping work while keeping it readable in
historical periods. Historical alignment reviews still show it, labelled
**(Archived)**.

Do not delete an archived ILO. Archiving is the reversible action; there is no
hard delete for a catalog entry that has been mapped against.

## Restore an archived ILO

Open the archived list and select **Restore**. The ILO returns to active status
and to its previous position.

## How ILOs connect to General Education CILOs

A General Education Course's CILOs are aligned to ILOs — not to program GOs.
Faculty author the mappings in the Course alignment workspace; you review them
at [General Education alignment](/gen-ed-coordinator/outcome-alignment/).
