The General Education Coordinator is the college-wide steward of General
Education and the owner of the Institutional Learning Outcome catalog.

<VideoGuide title="General Education Coordinator onboarding" duration="5:00" />

## Your scope

College-wide, and limited to **General Education**: Courses whose
**Course Scope** is General Education, plus the Institutional Learning Outcomes
those Courses support.

## What you manage

- **Institutional Learning Outcomes (ILOs)** — create, edit, reorder, archive, restore.
- **General Education Course assignments** — create, edit, reassign Faculty, deactivate, delete. College-wide, for any GE Course.
- **General Education Courses** — the GE catalog as it is exposed to you.
- **General Education analytics** — cross-program GE evidence.

## What you do not manage

:::caution
- **Rosters.** You assign Faculty to GE Courses; the assigned Faculty member maintains the roster. See [Course rosters](/faculty/course-rosters/).
- **Program-specific Course assignments.** Those belong to each Program Head.
- **Graduate Outcomes.** Owned by each Program Head.
- **On-behalf deployment.** Unlike the Dean, you do not publish on behalf of Faculty.
:::
