This is the college-wide view of how General Education CILOs connect to
Institutional Learning Outcomes.

## CILO to ILO

Each row is a General Education Course-assignment context. Each row shows its
active CILOs and the ILO each one is mapped to.

A GE CILO is aligned to an **ILO**. It is never expected to align to a Program
Graduate Outcome — that is the structural difference between a GE context and a
Program-specific one.

## Manifestation values

**Manifestation** is how strongly a CILO delivers an outcome. It is set per
mapping by the Faculty member and read by everyone else:

| Value | Reading |
| --- | --- |
| Introduced | The outcome appears on the Course, with light coverage |
| Reinforced | The outcome is developed further on the Course |
| Assessed | The outcome is evaluated directly on this Course |

Manifestation is recorded on the mapping, not on the outcome, because the same
outcome can be manifested differently in two Courses.

## What you can review

- Every GE alignment in the college.
- Which CILOs are unmapped.
- The manifestation value on each existing mapping.
- Readiness per context, and which contexts block publication.

## Who maintains mappings

:::caution
**Faculty maintain the mappings.** You review them. An unmapped CILO is normally
fixed by asking the assigned Faculty member to complete their alignment, not by
editing it centrally — a mapping records what that Course actually does.
:::

If a mapping is factually wrong and the Faculty member cannot correct it, raise
it through the documented correction path rather than silently editing it.

## Readiness

A context is **ready** when every active CILO satisfies the typed alignment rule.
Until then, publication of a GE Course evaluation is blocked. See
[I cannot publish an evaluation](/troubleshooting/cannot-publish-evaluation/).
