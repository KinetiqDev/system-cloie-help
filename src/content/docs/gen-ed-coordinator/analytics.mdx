General Education analytics is a **separate, cross-program** evidence path. It
is not a filtered view of Program Head analytics.

## What evidence is included

:::caution
The first release covers **Course-bound General Education evidence** only.
Program-wide deployments and other stakeholder evidence do not flow through this
path yet. An empty GE analytics view does not mean nothing was evaluated — check
whether the evidence was Course-bound in the first place.
:::

Every figure traces back to submitted responses. Nothing here counts planned or
scheduled evaluations.

## Choose the relevant scope or period

Use the analytics scope filters to select:

- the **academic term** you are evaluating;
- the General Education **Course** or evaluation you want to look at;
- the stakeholder source, where several exist.

Evidence sources are kept **separate**, never pooled. A GE figure drawn from
Student responses is not combined with Alumni or Industry Partner responses.

## Interpret the results

- **Exact values by period** and **Comparable runs** — only runs with comparable
  instruments and scales may be compared. Where comparability breaks, System
  CLOIE tells you rather than silently comparing.
- **Ratings and distribution** — the shape of answers, not a grade.
- **Qualitative summary** — a deterministic summary of submitted written answers.
  It is a description of what respondents wrote, not an interpretation.
- **AI-generated insight** — a bounded, non-persisted supplement over aggregate
  packets. No raw comments and no respondent identifiers cross that boundary, and
  it never replaces human judgement.

See [How to read CLOIE evidence](/concepts/analytics-and-evidence/) for what these
figures do and do not mean.
