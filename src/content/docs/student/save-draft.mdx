Your answers are saved **section by section** while an evaluation is in progress,
so you do not have to finish in one sitting.

## When drafts are saved

- Every time you move between sections.
- Whenever you leave and come back to the same evaluation.

The draft is private to you. Nobody — not your teacher, not the program, not an
administrator — can see a draft, and drafts are never counted as responses.

:::caution
**A draft is not a submission.** An evaluation only counts as answered once you
submit it. A draft that sits at the deadline is lost work.
:::

## Return to the evaluation

Select it again from **Evaluations**, or from **In-progress** on your
[dashboard](/student/dashboard/). You come back to the section you left.

## Deadline warning

Each open evaluation shows its **Deadline**. System CLOIE warns you as the
deadline approaches.

Once the deadline passes, the evaluation closes and the draft is no longer
submittable. There is no late submission and no administrative override —
plan for the deadline, not around it.

## If you are on a bad connection

Finish a section before moving. That is the unit the draft saves on.

## After you submit

Saving stops working. A submitted response rejects further draft saves and
further submissions — see
[Can I change a submitted evaluation?](/student/submit/#can-i-change-a-submitted-evaluation).
