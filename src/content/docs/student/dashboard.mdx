The **Dashboard** is your home page in System CLOIE. It answers one question:
*is there anything I need to do right now?*

## Available evaluations

Evaluations that are **Active** and that you are eligible for. Each one shows its
Course and its **Available from** / **Deadline** window.

If this list is empty, either nothing is open for you now or something upstream is
missing. Start at [I cannot see an evaluation](/troubleshooting/evaluation-not-visible/).

## In-progress evaluations

Evaluations you have started and not submitted. Your answers are saved as you
move between sections — see [Save an evaluation draft](/student/save-draft/).

:::caution
An in-progress evaluation is a **draft**. It is not a submission, and nobody can
see it. It does not count toward completion until you submit.
:::

## Academic placement warning

If your dashboard shows a placement warning, your account has no active-term
placement. Until that is recorded you cannot be added to a Course roster, and
Course evaluations will not reach you.

This is institutional, not something you can fix in the application. Ask the
Secretary or your program office — see
[My academic placement is missing](/troubleshooting/student-placement/).

## Previous submissions

A short list of what you have already submitted. The full record with dates is at
[Submission history](/student/history/).

## The four things on your navigation

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | What needs your attention now |
| **Evaluations** | Everything currently available to you |
| **Submission History** | What you have already submitted |
| **Profile** | Your personal, academic, and privacy details |
