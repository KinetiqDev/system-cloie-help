As a Student you use System CLOIE for one thing: completing the evaluations your
teachers have published for you.

<VideoGuide title="Student onboarding" duration="3:00" />

## What you use System CLOIE for

Answering an evaluation about a Course you took, and checking what you have
already submitted.

There are no grades here, no records of your academic history, and nothing that
affects your standing. Evaluations are not examinations.

## Your normal workflow

1. [Check your dashboard](/student/dashboard/) for anything open.
2. [Open the evaluation](/student/evaluations/).
3. [Answer it](/student/complete-evaluation/) — save as you go.
4. [Review and submit](/student/submit/).
5. Check [submission history](/student/history/) later.

## What you cannot do

- Create or change a Course roster.
- Write or edit CILOs or any learning outcome.
- Publish an evaluation.
- See analytics, or any other respondent's answers.
- See your own submitted answers after submitting.

:::caution
There is **no access code**. If you were told to enter a code to unlock an
evaluation, that is not how System CLOIE works — an evaluation reaches you when
your Faculty member publishes it and you are on the roster. See
[I cannot see an evaluation](/troubleshooting/evaluation-not-visible/).
:::

## Graduating Students

Graduating is not a separate role and not a separate account type. It changes
which evaluations are relevant to you. See
[Evaluations for graduating Students](/student/graduating/).
