**Evaluations** lists everything currently available to you.

## Why an evaluation appears here

All four must be true:

1. Your Faculty member published a Course evaluation.
2. You are on that Course's **roster**.
3. The evaluation's availability window is open.
4. You have an **active-term placement** and a complete profile.

If any one fails, the evaluation does not appear. The application does not show
you an evaluation you are not eligible for.

## Evaluation status

| Status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduled | Published, but the window has not opened. Come back later. |
| Active | Available now. Start it. |
| Closed | The window has ended. Nothing further to do. |

An evaluation you already submitted does not appear again — check
[submission history](/student/history/) instead.

## Open an evaluation

Select the evaluation from the list. It loads a **frozen copy** of the questions
as they were at publication; your Faculty member editing their tool afterwards
does not change what you are answering.

## If you expected an evaluation but cannot see it

Work through these in order:

1. **Is it open yet?** Check the **Available from** date. Many evaluations are
   published well before the window.
2. **Are you on the roster?** Your name has to be on the Course roster for that
   assignment. Ask your teacher — not the app.
3. **Do you have an active-term placement?** Check your dashboard for the warning.
4. **Did your teacher publish?** Published-but-Scheduled is the single most common
   cause.

Then read [I cannot see an evaluation](/troubleshooting/evaluation-not-visible/).
