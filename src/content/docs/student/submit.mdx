The review step is the last chance to change anything.

## Check your answers

The review page lists every section and your answers in full. Check:

- required questions are answered;
- rating questions are the ratings you meant to give;
- written answers say what you meant.

## Confirm submission

Select **Submit**. System CLOIE re-checks your required answers and your
eligibility one final time before accepting.

If something fails at this point, the form reports a **Validation Error** and
names what is wrong. Fix it and submit again — you have not submitted yet.

## What happens after submission

- **Your response has been recorded.**
- The evaluation leaves your available list and appears in
  [submission history](/student/history/).
- Your answers become part of your program's evidence, anonymized.

:::caution
Submission is final. Nobody at the college can reopen, edit, or withdraw your
response, and neither can you. Check your answers before you confirm.
:::

## Can I change a submitted evaluation?

No. One evaluation gets exactly one submitted response per person. A submitted
response rejects further draft saves and further submissions.

If you genuinely answered wrongly, contact your teacher — but be aware there is
no mechanism in the application to replace it.
