An evaluation is a short form split into sections. Nothing is recorded until you
save or submit.

<MediaPlaceholder caption="Student evaluation form showing a Likert statement with its scale and the How to answer guidance." />

## Before you begin

- The evaluation is **Active** now.
- You have not already submitted this evaluation.
- You are answering about **your own experience** of the Course.

## Read the introduction

The first screen explains what is being asked and how long it takes. Read it
before answering.

## Answer rating questions

Rate each statement on the scale provided. **How to answer** explains what the
scale points mean for this evaluation — read it once rather than guessing from
the numbers alone.

There is no right answer, and a middling rating is a real answer. Anonymized
responses are what your program uses to improve the Course.

## Rate CILOs when included

Some evaluations ask you to rate the Course's **Course Intended Learning
Outcomes** directly. These are the statements your teacher wrote describing what
the Course is meant to produce.

Judge what actually happened in your section, not what the Course catalog promises
or what other sections did.

## Answer written questions

Open-ended prompts want your own words. Specific examples are more useful to your
program than general remarks.

## Move through sections

Use the section navigation to move back and forth. You can revisit any section
before submitting.

## Save a draft

Your answers save section by section while the evaluation is in progress. See
[Save an evaluation draft](/student/save-draft/) for exactly what is kept.

## Fix incomplete required answers

Required questions are marked. If you try to continue past one, the form stops
and names what is missing — a **Validation Error** tells you which items are
**Not answered**.

Go back and complete them. You cannot submit around a required question.

## Review your answers

The final step shows everything together. Read it. This is the last point at
which you can change anything.

## Submit

Select **Submit**. See [Review and submit your evaluation](/student/submit/) for
what confirmation you get and what happens next.

## After submission

- Your response is final. **Responses are final after submission.**
- You will see **Your response has been recorded**.
- Your name is never shown to your teacher alongside your answers. They see
  anonymized results only.
