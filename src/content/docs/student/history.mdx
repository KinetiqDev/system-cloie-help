**Submission History** is your permanent record of what you answered and when.

## What appears in History

Every evaluation you have submitted, with:

- the Course or evaluation name;
- the date and time you submitted;
- the **Submitted** status.

Drafts do not appear. An evaluation you started and never submitted is not in
your history, because nothing was answered.

## Open a submitted response

Select a row to read what you submitted.

## Why answers are read-only

:::caution
Submitted responses are locked by design. That is what makes the evidence usable:
a program has to be able to trust that a response recorded in a given term is the
response that respondent actually gave in that term.
:::

You can read your submission; you cannot change it.

## Your history across roles

If you later become an **Alumni** — see
[Transition a graduated Student to Alumni](/secretary/student-to-alumni/) —
your Student submissions stay in your record exactly as they were. Alumni-targeted
evaluations appear in your Alumni
[submission history](/alumni/history/).

## Graduating Students

Finishing your degree does not clear your history. See
[Evaluations for graduating Students](/student/graduating/).
