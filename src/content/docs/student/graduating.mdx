If you are finishing your degree this term, an extra evaluation may appear that
your classmates in earlier years never saw.

## Graduating Student is not a separate CLOIE role

There are **eight** roles in System CLOIE. Graduating Student is not one of them.

You keep the **Student** role. What changes is your account's graduation status,
which System CLOIE uses to decide which evaluations are relevant to you. Nothing
about your sign-in, your permissions, or your history changes.

## Why a graduating evaluation may appear

A graduating evaluation targets Students in your program who are completing their
degree in the active term. It asks about your overall program experience rather
than one Course, which is why it does not come from a Course roster.

It may be:

- a **Program-wide** evaluation targeting your program's graduating Students;
- a final-term Course evaluation from a Course you are taking.

Check the evaluation's title and Course on the card before you start — they tell
you which it is.

## Complete the evaluation

Nothing about graduating changes the mechanics. Open it from
[Evaluations](/student/evaluations/), answer it, and
[submit](/student/submit/) it like any other.

Two things worth knowing:

- **Answer about your actual experience.** A graduating evaluation is often the
  last evidence a program collects from its own graduates, and your program reads
  it.
- **Watch the deadline.** Graduating evaluations are often scheduled tightly
  around term end. Check the **Deadline** before you start.

## After you graduate

Your Student account becomes an **Alumni** account after the Secretary completes
the transition. Until that happens you remain a Student, and any further
evaluation still reaches you as a Student.

Your existing submissions are preserved unchanged — see
[View your submitted evaluations](/student/history/) and
[Transition a graduated Student to Alumni](/secretary/student-to-alumni/).
