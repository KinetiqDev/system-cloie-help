Read these six pages once and System CLOIE stops being confusing.

1. [What is System CLOIE?](/start/what-is-system-cloie/) — what the application is for and how information moves through it.
2. [Choose your role](/start/choose-your-role/) — the eight roles and what each one changes.
3. [Sign in to System CLOIE](/start/sign-in/) — which entrance applies to you, and what happens after sign-in.
4. [Switch your active role](/start/switch-active-role/) — only needed if you hold more than one role.
5. [Complete your profile](/start/complete-your-profile/) — the gate that stops you entering a workspace.
6. [Understand access and permissions](/start/understand-access/) — why you can see a page but not change it.

Then go to the [role guide for your own role](/start/choose-your-role/).
