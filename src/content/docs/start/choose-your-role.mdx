Your role decides three things: which workspace you enter, which pages exist
inside it, and what you are allowed to change. It does not decide who you are —
that is your account.

<VideoGuide title="How roles work" duration="2:00" />

## What a role changes

- The **navigation** you see.
- The **scope** of your data — a Program Head sees only their authorized programs, a Dean sees the whole college.
- The **actions** available on a page. Viewing a page never implies you can edit it; see [Understand access and permissions](/start/understand-access/).

## Available roles

| Role | Owns | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Secretary | Accounts, academic calendar, programs, Courses, institutional evaluation tools | [Secretary guide](/secretary/) |
| College Dean | College-wide structure and readiness oversight | [Dean guide](/dean/) |
| General Education Coordinator | Institutional Learning Outcomes, General Education assignments | [Coordinator guide](/gen-ed-coordinator/) |
| Program Head | Graduate Outcomes, program Course assignments, program-wide evaluations, analytics | [Program Head guide](/program-head/) |
| Faculty Member | Course rosters, CILOs, Course evaluations | [Faculty guide](/faculty/) |
| Student | Completing Course-bound evaluations | [Student guide](/student/) |
| Alumni | Completing Alumni-targeted evaluations | [Alumni guide](/alumni/) |
| Industry Partner | Completing Industry Partner-targeted evaluations | [Industry Partner guide](/industry-partner/) |

**Graduating Student is not a role.** It is a condition on a Student account that
changes which evaluations are relevant. See [Evaluations for graduating Students](/student/graduating/).

## If you have more than one role

One account can hold several roles, but exactly one is **active** at a time. The
active role is the only one System CLOIE authorizes. Use
[Switch your active role](/start/switch-active-role/) to change it.

## Why a role may not be available

- Internal roles (Secretary, Dean, Program Head, General Education Coordinator) are **pre-provisioned** by the Secretary. They are never self-assigned.
- Faculty, Student, Alumni, and Industry Partner roles can be claimed, but only after a successful sign-in with an account that matches an existing record.
- A Faculty role also has to be approved by the Secretary before it opens.
- An Alumni or Industry Partner account stays **pending** until institutional verification finishes.

If a role you expect is missing, read [My role is missing](/troubleshooting/role-missing/).
