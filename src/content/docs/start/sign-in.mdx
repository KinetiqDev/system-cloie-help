You reach the System CLOIE sign-in page from the main site. Which entrance you
use depends on whether your account is internal to ACD or external.

## Before you sign in

You need:

- an **ACD institutional email address** for internal roles (Secretary, Dean, Program Head, General Education Coordinator, Faculty, Student);
- **any valid email address** for Alumni and Industry Partner;
- access to that inbox.

## Internal ACD users

Choose the **staff**, **student**, or **Faculty registration** entrance that
matches your role. Internal roles sign in with **Google only** — a password,
one-time code, or recovery link will not open an internal workspace, even for
the same email address.

Your account is matched by exact, normalized email address. If the address you
sign in with does not match an existing System CLOIE account, you will be asked
to register rather than admitted.

## Alumni and Industry Partners

Alumni and Industry Partner are the only roles that may use **either Google or
email and password**. The external sign-in page offers both.

- If you already signed up with Google, keep using Google.
- If you registered with an email and password, use that password.
- If you registered with one method and try the other, sign-in is refused — use the
  method you registered with, or use **Forgot password** to set a new one.

New external registrations begin in a **pending verification** state and cannot
enter a workspace until the Secretary's verification is recorded.

## What happens after sign-in

1. System CLOIE checks which roles your account holds.
2. If you hold more than one, you are sent to [role selection](/start/switch-active-role/).
3. If your profile for the active role is incomplete, you are sent to onboarding.
4. If everything is in order, you land on that role's dashboard.
