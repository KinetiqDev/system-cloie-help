If your account holds more than one role, System CLOIE asks you to pick one each
time you enter. Only the active role is authorized — the others simply wait.

<VideoGuide title="How roles work" duration="2:00" />

## When this applies

- You are both a Faculty member and a Student.
- You are both an Alumni and an Industry Partner.
- You are a Program Head for two different programs — but note that this is handled
  by [Select the program you are working with](/program-head/select-program/), not by the role selector.

## What switching roles changes

- The workspace you land on.
- The navigation items you see.
- Which pages authorize for you.

## What switching roles does not change

- It does not create or remove a role.
- It does not change your profile information in other roles.
- It is not impersonation. Nobody's actions are recorded as yours.

## Switch roles

1. Open the **role selector** from the workspace switcher in the header.
2. Select the **Switch to …** button on the role you want to work in.
3. The application reloads into that role's workspace.

If you sign in and the selector offers only one role, your account holds one role.
See [My role is missing](/troubleshooting/role-missing/).
