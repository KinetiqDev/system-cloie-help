System CLOIE will not open a workspace until the profile required by your active
role is complete. This is deliberate: profile data is what makes an evaluation
answerable later.

## Why profile information is required

- A Student placement has to exist before that Student can be rostered.
- A Faculty record has to exist before a Course assignment can point at it.
- An Alumni or Industry Partner organization has to exist before a deployment can target them.
- Names are taken from your sign-in provider when your account is first linked, so your profile name normally matches your real name.

## Student profile

Basic details, program and major where applicable, year level and section, and
your academic placement for the current active term.

:::caution
A Student without an active placement can still sign in, but the dashboard shows a
placement warning and no evaluations become available. See
[My academic placement is missing](/troubleshooting/student-placement/).
:::

## Faculty profile

Personal and academic information, the program you are affiliated with, and your
teaching details. Being affiliated with a program does **not** give you access to
every Course in it — access comes from a Course assignment. See
[What your Program affiliation means](/faculty/#what-your-program-affiliation-means).

## Alumni profile

Personal details plus the degree and graduation information used to verify that
you are an alumnus. Verification status is shown on the profile page.

## Industry Partner profile

Personal details plus **organization information** — organization name and your
primary program affiliation — used both for verification and to decide which
evaluations you are invited to.

## What happens if required information is missing

Your active role resolves to **no role**, so every role guard fails closed and
you are returned to onboarding. Your account and its other roles are untouched;
only the incomplete role is withheld. Cancelling onboarding removes only the role
that was requested, and only while its required profile is still absent.
