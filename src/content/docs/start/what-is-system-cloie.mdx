System CLOIE is the learning outcomes evaluation platform of Assumption College of
Davao. It records what a Course is meant to produce, gathers evidence that it
did, and keeps that evidence for the programs and the college.

This page explains the shape of the application. It does not explain any single
task — your role guide does that.

<VideoGuide title="System CLOIE overview" duration="3:00" />

## What System CLOIE helps ACD do

Every academic program writes down its **Graduate Outcomes (GOs)**. Every Course
writes down its own **Course Intended Learning Outcomes (CILOs)**. The college
also holds a small set of **Institutional Learning Outcomes (ILOs)** that apply
across the whole college.

System CLOIE stores those three kinds of outcomes, records which CILOs support
which broader outcomes, and then collects evaluations that answer the question
"did this Course actually deliver what it promised?"

## Who uses System CLOIE

Eight roles, described in full on [Choose your role](/start/choose-your-role/):

- **Secretary** — sets up accounts, the academic calendar, programs, and Courses.
- **College Dean** — oversees the whole college.
- **General Education Coordinator** — owns Institutional Learning Outcomes and General Education.
- **Program Head** — owns Graduate Outcomes and publishes program-wide evaluations.
- **Faculty Member** — prepares a Course: roster, CILOs, and the Course evaluation.
- **Student**, **Alumni**, **Industry Partner** — complete the evaluations they are invited to.

## What System CLOIE does not do

- It is not a gradebook. No marks, no grades, no transcript.
- It is not a class-list system of record. The Course roster is what the Secretary
  and the Program Head prepare, and Faculty maintain inside it.
- It does not email anyone for you. Notices about published evaluations appear
  inside the application.

## How information moves through CLOIE

1. The Secretary creates accounts, programs, Courses, and the academic calendar.
2. A Program Head or General Education Coordinator assigns a Faculty member to a Course.
3. Faculty adds Students to the Course roster, writes the CILOs, and maps them to broader outcomes.
4. Faculty — or a Program Head, Dean, or Secretary on their behalf — publishes an evaluation.
5. Students on the roster answer it.
6. The Faculty member and Program Head read the results as evidence, and the Program Head uses it for analytics.

The [Course evaluation lifecycle](/workflows/course-evaluation/) page walks through
each of these handoffs in plain language.
