## Short task walkthroughs

Task videos are only listed here once they exist. Task pages carry a
[video slot](/faculty/publish-course-evaluation/) next to their written steps, and
render as a quiet placeholder until a URL is supplied.

## Adding a video later

Set `videoUrl` in a page's frontmatter to a YouTube watch, share, or embed URL
(or a bare video id), and optionally set `videoDuration`. The placeholder becomes
an embedded player with a link out to YouTube. No video ever autoplays.
