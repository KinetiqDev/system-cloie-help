**Problem.** You registered as an Industry Partner and your account shows
**Pending**, or you cannot reach the workspace.

## Check this first

1. You are signing in with the **external** entrance.
2. Your **Organization Name** matches how the college records it.
3. Your **primary program affiliation** is the program that contacted you.

## Why this can happen

- The organization details could not be confirmed against institutional records.
- The evaluation you were expecting belongs to a **different program**. An
  evaluation is only offered to accounts whose primary affiliation matches the
  publishing program.

:::caution
Support for a partner affiliated with **several** programs at once is still an
open policy question. Your account carries one primary affiliation, and that
single field decides which evaluations you see.
:::

## What you can do

- Correct the **Organization Name** or **primary program affiliation** and save.
- Sign in periodically; pending accounts are released when verification completes.

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the college registry or the **Program Head** who requested the evaluation.
If they expected you to receive an evaluation and your affiliation is right, the
gap is on their side — not something to work around.
