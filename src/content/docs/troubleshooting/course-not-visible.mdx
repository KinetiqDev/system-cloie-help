**Problem.** A Course you are teaching does not appear on **My Course Rosters**.

## Check this first

1. You are signed in with the **Faculty Member** role active.
2. The academic period shown by the filters matches the term you are teaching.

## Why this can happen

:::caution
**Faculty Program affiliation is not access.** Being affiliated with a program
does not give you access to every Course in it. You see a Course only when you
hold an **active Course assignment** for it.
:::

Other causes:

- The assignment has not been created yet, or was created for a different period,
  year level, or section.
- The assignment exists but is **inactive**.
- You are signed in as a different role — a Program Head who also teaches sees
  Course assignments only in their program context.

## What you can do

- Clear the status filters and switch the period.
- Ask a colleague who can see the assignment to confirm it exists and is active.

## When to contact an administrator

Ask whoever owns the Course:

| Course scope | Who creates the assignment |
| --- | --- |
| Program-specific | Your **Program Head**, or the **Dean** |
| General Education | The **General Education Coordinator**, or the **Dean** |

See [Program-specific Course assignments](/program-head/course-assignments/).
