**Problem.** The **Publish Evaluation** action will not go through.

## Check this first

1. Every **active CILO** in the Course assignment has an outcome **and** a
   manifestation value.
2. You hold the assignment you are publishing against.
3. The tool is saved — publication works from a saved version.

## Why this can happen

:::caution
**The alignment gate is the usual reason.** Publication of a Course-bound
evaluation is blocked until every active CILO satisfies the typed alignment rule.
This is not a warning and it cannot be overridden.

It exists because a rating is only evidence about an outcome if you recorded which
outcome it measured. Publishing first would collect responses that cannot be
attributed to anything.
:::

Other causes:

- You are Faculty and do not hold the assignment. The **Dean** and the **Program
  Head** can publish on your behalf; the Secretary can too.
- An archived CILO is still being treated as active.
- The tool has unsaved changes.

## What you can do

1. Open the Course's **Course Alignment** workspace.
2. Find the unmapped or incomplete CILOs — see
   [My CILO alignment is incomplete](/troubleshooting/missing-outcome-alignment/).
3. Save each mapping, then return to publishing.

## When to contact an administrator

If every CILO is aligned and publication still fails, you are probably not the
deployer for that assignment. Ask your **Program Head** or the **Dean** to publish
on your behalf — see
[Course evaluation on behalf of Faculty](/dean/evaluation-tools/).
