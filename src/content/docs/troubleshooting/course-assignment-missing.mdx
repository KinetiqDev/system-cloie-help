**Problem.** A Course is not on **My Course Rosters** even though you are teaching
it this term.

## Check this first

1. The academic period filter matches the term you are teaching.
2. The status filter is not hiding **Inactive** assignments.
3. The Course exists in the catalog under the scope you expect.

## Why this can happen

- Nobody has created the assignment yet for that period.
- The assignment was created for a different **period, year level, or section**.
- The assignment is **inactive** from a previous term.
- The Course's **scope** is not what you assume — a General Education Course is
  assigned by the General Education Coordinator, not by your Program Head.

## What you can do

- Clear the filters and re-check.
- Look the Course up in the catalog to confirm its code, title, and scope.

## When to contact an administrator

| Course scope | Who creates the assignment |
| --- | --- |
| Program-specific | Your **Program Head**, or the **Dean** |
| General Education | The **General Education Coordinator**, or the **Dean** |

The Secretary can see assignments but cannot create one.
