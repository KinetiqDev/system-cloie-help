**Problem.** You cannot add or remove anyone from a Course roster.

## Check this first

- Whether an evaluation has been **published** from that assignment and is still
  **Scheduled** or **Active**.

## Why this can happen

:::caution
Publishing locks **ordinary roster writes** for that assignment. At publication,
one respondent assignment is created per eligible roster member, minus recorded
exclusions. Letting the roster change under a live evaluation would mean the
evidence had been collected from one population and reported for another.
:::

## What you can do

Use the evaluation's **Exclusions & late inclusion** controls instead:

- **Active exclusion** removes someone from the current deployment without editing
  the roster.
- **Late inclusion** adds someone while the window is still open.

These are reversible within the window and are recorded as explicit decisions.

## After the evaluation closes

Ordinary roster writes are unlocked again. Historical changes still apply to
future evaluations — the respondent who was excluded from the closed evaluation
will be included in the next one unless you change the roster.

## When to contact an administrator

If no evaluation is running and the roster is still locked, or if you need a
correction that would misrepresent who answered, contact the **Secretary** or the
**Dean** rather than working around it.
