**Problem.** Your Alumni or Industry Partner account rejects the email and
password you registered with, or rejects Google when you expected it to work.

## Check this first

1. You are on the **external** sign-in page. That page offers both methods; the
   internal pages do not.
2. You are using the method you **registered** with.
3. Your address matches the one on the account exactly.

## Why this can happen

Alumni and Industry Partner are the only roles that may use email-password
alongside Google — but **one account is tied to the method it was created with.**

- An account registered with a password will not accept Google sign-in.
- An account linked to Google will not accept a password.
- An account created by the Secretary may have been created without any password
  at all, in which case only Google works.

## What you can do

- Try the **other** method once.
- If you registered with a password and cannot remember it, use **Forgot password**
  on the external page and set a new one.
- Do **not** register a second account with a different address — you will end up
  with two half-records and neither will work.

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the college registry if **Forgot password** does not reach your address, or
if your address on the account is not what you expect. Your account may have been
created by the Secretary rather than by you.
