**Problem.** You sign in successfully but System CLOIE says your account is
inactive or deactivated.

## Check this first

- The message names the account as **Inactive** or **Deactivated**.
- You are not signed in as a different person — see
  [I signed into the wrong account](/troubleshooting/sign-in/account-match/).

## Why this can happen

The Secretary deactivated the account deliberately: a person who left, a
duplicate record that was merged, or a record created in error.

## What you can do

Nothing yourself — deactivation is an account-level decision made by the
Secretary.

## What has *not* changed

:::caution
Deactivation does **not** delete anything. Evaluations you already submitted, your
submission history, and the responses other people received all remain intact.
Deactivation stops sign-in; it does not erase evidence.
:::

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the **Secretary** and ask for the account to be set back to **Active**.
See [Manage users](/secretary/users/).
