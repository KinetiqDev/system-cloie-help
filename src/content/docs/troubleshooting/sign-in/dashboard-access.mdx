**Problem.** Your sign-in succeeds, but you never reach your role's dashboard.

## Check this first

1. Read the notice you land on. It names the reason.
2. Check whether you hold **more than one role** — in that case System CLOIE is
   correctly asking you to choose an active role.
3. Check whether your **profile** for that role is complete.

## Why this can happen

- **Role selection is pending.** A multi-role account must pick an active role
  before anything authorizes.
- **A profile gate is unsatisfied.** Your active role resolves to *no role* until
  the required profile artifact exists, so every role guard fails closed.
- **The account is pending verification** — the external case.
- **The account is inactive.**
- **A Student has no active-term placement** — a dashboard-only warning rather
  than a hard block.

## What you can do

- Complete onboarding if you were sent there.
- Select an active role if you were asked to.
- Finish the missing profile fields, then sign in again.
- For a Student placement warning, see
  [My academic placement is missing](/troubleshooting/student-placement/).

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the **Secretary** if onboarding will not complete, or if a Faculty role
you have been approved for still does not open.
