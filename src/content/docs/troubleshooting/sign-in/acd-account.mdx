**Problem.** You enter your ACD email address, choose the internal entrance, and
System CLOIE refuses the sign-in — or signs you in and never reaches the
application.

## Check this first

1. You are using the **internal entrance** (staff, student, or Faculty
   registration) — not the external one.
2. You are signing in with **Google**, not with a password.
3. The email address you typed is **exactly** your ACD address.

## Why this can happen

- **A password was used for an internal role.** Internal roles — Secretary, Dean,
  Program Head, General Education Coordinator, Faculty, Student — may only enter
  from a Google sign-in. A password, one-time code, or recovery session for the
  same address is refused, even though the address is correct.
- **The address does not match an existing account.** Accounts are matched by
  exact, normalized email. A different address, an alias, or a typo creates a new
  registration instead of an admission.
- **The account is inactive.** A deactivated account cannot sign in.
- **The account does not exist yet.** Internal roles are pre-provisioned by the
  Secretary; they are never self-assigned.

## What you can do

- Sign in with Google from the internal entrance.
- Sign out of Google completely, then try again in a private window.
- Confirm your exact ACD address with your department.
- If you are registering for the first time, use the entrance that matches your
  role and let the account be created.

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the **Secretary** if your Google sign-in succeeds but you are then told
your account does not exist, is inactive, or has no role. The Secretary owns
account creation, activation, and role assignment — see
[Create a user account](/secretary/create-user/).
