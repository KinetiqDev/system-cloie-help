**Problem.** You sign in with your own email and land on an account that is not
yours, or you cannot find your own record.

## Check this first

- Which email address you actually signed in with.
- Whether you are using a shared or personal device with a Google account still
  signed in.

## Why this can happen

- **A different Google identity was already signed in** and you never chose your
  own on the Google screen.
- **Addresses are matched exactly.** Accounts are keyed on exact, normalized email
  — a plausible alternative address is a different account, not a match.
- **Your account name came from Google.** The canonical account name is taken
  from the Google profile when the account is first created or first linked, so a
  personal Google display name can carry through.

## What you can do

1. Sign out of System CLOIE and of Google completely.
2. Sign in again and pick the correct Google identity on the Google screen.
3. If the identity is right but the account content is not, the address on your
   record is probably wrong — continue below.

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the **Secretary**. Do not edit the record yourself even if you can. An
account holds submitted responses that are attributed to whoever held the record
at the time — changing the address without fixing the underlying match corrupts
that trail.
