**Problem.** A CILO has no outcome, or the alignment page shows a context as not
ready.

## Check this first

1. Open the Course's **Course Alignment** workspace.
2. Look for CILOs shown as **Unanswered** or without a manifestation value.

## Why this can happen

- A CILO was created after the alignment was last saved.
- A CILO was restored from **archive** and came back active without a mapping.
- The Course's **scope** is wrong, so the outcome list offered was the wrong family
  of outcomes.

## What you can do

For each unmapped CILO:

1. Choose the outcome it maps to — a **Graduate Outcome** for a Program-specific
   Course, an **Institutional Learning Outcome** for a General Education Course.
2. Choose the manifestation value: **Introduced**, **Reinforced**, or **Assessed**.
3. Save.

A CILO can map to more than one outcome, and one outcome can be supported by CILOs
in many Courses.

:::caution
One CILO can carry many Likert questions. If you want more statements, add
questions to the same CILO rather than creating a near-duplicate CILO — a duplicate
creates a second unmapped CILO and a second thing to fix.
:::

## If the outcome list looks wrong

The Course's **Course Scope** decides which outcomes are offered. A Program-specific
Course offers GOs; a General Education Course offers ILOs. Ask the Secretary to
correct the scope — see [Programs, majors, and Courses](/concepts/programs-majors-courses/).

## Why this matters before publication

Readiness is not cosmetic. It gates publication — see
[I cannot publish an evaluation](/troubleshooting/cannot-publish-evaluation/).
