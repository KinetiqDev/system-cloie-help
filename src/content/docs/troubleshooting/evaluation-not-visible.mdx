**Problem.** An evaluation you expect does not appear on your Evaluations page.

## Check this first

Work these in order — they are the actual causes, most common first.

1. **Is it published and open?** A tool saved by your teacher is not an
   evaluation. It has to be published, and the window has to be open.
2. **Are you on the roster?** Membership is per Course assignment, per section.
3. **Do you have an active-term placement** and a complete profile?
4. **Have you already submitted it?** A submitted evaluation leaves your list.
5. **Are you the right kind of respondent?** Course-bound evaluations go to
   Students; Alumni and Industry Partner deployments go only to verified external
   accounts.

## Why this can happen

System CLOIE does not show you an evaluation you are not eligible for — there is
no greyed-out entry, no error, nothing. Eligibility is rechecked at the moment you
open one, so an evaluation can also disappear between being listed and being
opened.

## What you can do

- Check the **Available from** date: see
  [The evaluation has not opened yet](/troubleshooting/evaluation-not-open-yet/).
- Confirm you are on the roster for that specific assignment.
- Fix a dashboard placement warning, then reload — see
  [My academic placement is missing](/troubleshooting/student-placement/).

## If you are a Student

Your teacher publishes the evaluation; nobody else can. Ask them whether it is
published and when it opens.

## If you are Alumni or an Industry Partner

Check that your account is verified — a **pending** account sees nothing. Then
check your program affiliation: an evaluation is only offered to accounts
affiliated with the publishing program. See
[My Alumni account is still pending](/troubleshooting/alumni-verification/) and
[My Industry Partner account is still pending](/troubleshooting/industry-verification/).

## When the problem continues

Contact your teacher or Program Head for a Course evaluation, and the college
registry for an external account problem. Do not assume it is a system fault
before those two checks.
