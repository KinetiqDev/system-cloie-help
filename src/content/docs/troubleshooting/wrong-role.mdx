**Problem.** You hold more than one role and System CLOIE opened the workspace for
the one you did not want.

## Check this first

- Whether your account really holds both roles.
- Which role is marked **Current** in the role selector.

## Why this can happen

Only one role is **active** at a time, and it is the only one authorized. If your
account holds several, System CLOIE asks you to choose — and remembering a
previous choice can send you back to it.

## What you can do

1. Open the **role selector** from the header.
2. Select **Switch to …** on the role you want.
3. The workspace reloads with that role active.

Switching does not grant or remove a role — it only changes which one is
authorized. See [Switch your active role](/start/switch-active-role/).

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the **Secretary** if a role you need is not in the selector at all. The
selector only lists assigned roles; it cannot add one.
