**Problem.** You select **Submit** and the evaluation will not go through.

## Check this first

1. Every required question is answered — see
   [I have incomplete required answers](/troubleshooting/incomplete-required-answers/).
2. The evaluation is still **Active**. Status is rechecked at submission.
3. You are eligible right now: account active, profile complete, active-term
   placement, on the roster, not excluded.

## Why this can happen

:::caution
Submission re-validates both completeness **and** eligibility at the moment you
confirm. Something that was true when you opened the evaluation may no longer be:
the window may have closed, an exclusion may have been applied, or your account
state may have changed.
:::

Other causes:

- **Submission failed** — a transient network or server error. Nothing was saved
  as submitted, so you can try again.
- **You already submitted it** — see
  [System CLOIE says I already submitted](/troubleshooting/already-submitted/).

## What you can do

- Check your completion status and the evaluation's status on the review page.
- Fix any validation error and submit again.
- Wait a moment and retry if you saw **Submission failed** — the answer is still in
  your draft.

## When to contact an administrator

If the evaluation is Active, you are eligible, every required answer is present,
and it still refuses, report the evaluation name and the exact message. Your draft
is preserved until submission, so nothing is lost while you report it.
