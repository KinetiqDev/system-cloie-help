**Problem.** System CLOIE shows an **access denied** or **unauthorized** message
for a page you believe belongs to your role.

## Check this first

1. Which role is **active** right now?
2. Is the page inside your **scope** — your program, or your assigned Courses?
3. Is the feature simply not implemented for anyone yet?

## Why this can happen

- Your **active role** does not own that route. Internal roles have separate
  route trees with no impersonation between them.
- Your **scope** does not include that program or Course.
- Your **profile gate** is not satisfied, so the active role resolves to no role.
- The route exists but the feature is **deferred** — for example Dean Analytics
  and Dean Reports.

## What you can do

- Switch to the correct active role and try again:
  [Switch your active role](/start/switch-active-role/).
- Confirm the program or Course is within your scope.
- If a page is listed in a role's guide but refuses you, treat it as not
  implemented rather than as a bug to work around.

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the **Secretary** if your scope is genuinely correct — managed programs
and Course assignments are the two things most often recorded incorrectly.
