**Problem.** Your Student dashboard shows an academic placement warning, and no
Course evaluations appear for you.

## Check this first

- Which academic period is **active** right now.
- Your **program, major, year level, and section** on your profile.

## Why this can happen

- **No active academic period exists.** Placement is evaluated against the active
  term; with no active term, Students sit in deferred enrollment.
- **Rollover has not run** for this term yet.
- **Your profile is incomplete**, which the Secretary needs in order to place you.
- You are a **new Student** created while no period was active.

:::caution
Placement is institutional, set by the Secretary or by
[term rollover](/secretary/term-rollover/). You cannot create or change it yourself
in System CLOIE.
:::

## What you can do

- Complete any missing profile fields first — placement follows a complete profile.
- Ask your program office to confirm you have been placed for this term.

## What changes once you are placed

Once placement is recorded, you become **evaluation-eligible** and any Course
evaluation already published for a roster you are on will appear. You do not need
to ask for it again.

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the **Secretary** (or your program office) if the term has started and you
have a complete profile but no placement.
