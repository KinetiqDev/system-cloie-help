**Problem.** System CLOIE will not let you continue or submit, and shows a
**Validation Error** naming items as **Not answered**.

## Check this first

The error message names the specific items. Read it rather than guessing.

## Why this can happen

Required questions must be answered before the form will advance. Some questions
also need a value that counts as an answer — a Likert question with no rating
selected is unanswered even if you read it.

## What you can do

1. Go to the section named in the error.
2. Complete every required question. Look for an unselected rating scale.
3. For written questions, check that the field is not empty.
4. Return to the review step and submit again.

:::caution
You cannot submit around a required question. The final submission step
re-checks completeness, so an evaluation that looks complete but is missing one
required answer will still be refused.
:::

## When you have answered everything

If the error persists and every required item is filled, the form may not have
registered the change — move to another section and back, then retry. If it still
fails, report the evaluation name and the question.

See [Review and submit your evaluation](/student/submit/).
