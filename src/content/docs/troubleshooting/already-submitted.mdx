**Problem.** You try to open or submit an evaluation and System CLOIE says you
have already submitted it.

## Check this first

- Your [submission history](/student/history/) — the evaluation may be there with
  a submission date.

## Why this can happen

:::caution
Exactly one submitted response exists per evaluation, and the rule is enforced in
the database, not only in the interface. A submitted response rejects further
draft saves and further submissions.

This is what makes the evidence trustworthy: the response recorded for a term is
the response you actually gave in that term.
:::

If you are certain you never submitted, you may have confirmed the dialog without
intending to — the review screen's submit action is final.

## What you can do

- Open the row in [submission history](/student/history/) to see what was recorded
  and when.
- If it was submitted in error, contact the person who published the evaluation.
  Be clear that the application provides no way for you to replace it.

## What cannot be done

:::caution
Nobody at the college can reopen, edit, or withdraw your response through System
CLOIE. Do not expect an administrator override — there is none.
:::

If the submission is factually wrong, the resolution is a documented correction
outside the application, agreed with the publishing role.
