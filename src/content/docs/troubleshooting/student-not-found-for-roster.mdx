**Problem.** You cannot add a Student to your Course roster, or a CSV import says
the Student is **Ambiguous** or **No match**.

## Check this first

1. The Student account is **active** — not deactivated.
2. The Student has a **complete profile**.
3. The Student has an **active-term placement**.
4. The name you typed matches their account name.

## Why this can happen

:::caution
Roster members are matched **by name**. There are no Student IDs in a roster. A
name that matches more than one Student is reported as **Ambiguous** and is never
resolved automatically — the system will not guess which person you meant.
:::

- **Ineligible** members can be added, but will not become evaluation-eligible
  until their record is fixed. The row shows why.
- **Invalid name** means the row does not resolve to any account at all.
- **Already Enrolled** means that Student is already on this roster.

## What you can do

- Use **Add one Student** and check the returned program, major, and year level
  before confirming.
- On import, resolve each **Ambiguous** row deliberately — open the candidate and
  pick the right one.
- Add the Student anyway if they belong; they become eligible once the Secretary
  fixes their record.

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the **Secretary** if a Student has no account, an incomplete profile, or
no placement. Placement is institutional — see
[My academic placement is missing](/troubleshooting/student-placement/).
