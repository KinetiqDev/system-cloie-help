**Problem.** A program you head is absent from the Program Head program selector,
or its pages return access denied.

## Check this first

1. You are signed in with the **Program Head** role active.
2. You are not simply looking at a different program — confirm the active one.

## Why this can happen

:::caution
Program Head authority comes from the **managed programs** recorded on your
account. It is resolved server-side on every request. A URL, a cookie, or a
remembered preference never establishes authority — so a program that is not on
your account is genuinely unreachable.
:::

The program may also have been removed from your account, or assigned to a
different Program Head.

## What you can do

- Sign out and sign in again, then check the program selector.
- Confirm with your colleagues whether the program is listed on your account.

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the **Secretary** to have the program added under **Managed Programs** —
see [Manage users](/secretary/users/). Also raise it with the Program Head of the
program in question, since two people may be assigned to it.
