**Problem.** Your answers will not save, or you have lost them.

## Check this first

1. Whether you have already **submitted** the evaluation.
2. Whether the evaluation is still **Active** rather than **Closed**.
3. Whether the browser is reporting a connection problem.

## Why this can happen

- **The response is already submitted.** A submitted response rejects further
  draft saves. See
  [System CLOIE says I already submitted](/troubleshooting/already-submitted/).
- **The window closed.** A closed evaluation stops accepting saves.
- **The evaluation is no longer eligible for you.** Eligibility is rechecked
  against your account state, placement, roster membership, and the window.

:::caution
Saving happens **section by section**. If a section appears to have lost its
answers, check whether you actually moved off it — moving sections is what
triggers the save.
:::

## What you can do

- Reload the page and reopen the evaluation from your dashboard.
- Move off a section explicitly (select the next section) rather than closing the tab, so the save is committed.
- Keep one section finished before moving on a poor connection.

## When to contact an administrator

If the evaluation is still Active, you are not eligible-excluded, and a section
refuses to save repeatedly, report it with the evaluation name and the section.
Do not try to reconstruct the answers elsewhere — a submitted response must be
this one.
