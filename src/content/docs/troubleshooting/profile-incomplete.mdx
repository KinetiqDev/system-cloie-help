**Problem.** You sign in and are sent to onboarding instead of your workspace,
or a notice says your profile is incomplete.

## Check this first

- Which **active role** you selected. Profile requirements differ per role.
- Whether you can save each section without an error.

## Why this can happen

System CLOIE resolves your active role against a **profile gate**. Until the
required artifact for that role exists, the role resolves to no role and every
role guard fails closed. This applies to Students, Faculty, Alumni, and Industry
Partners.

## What you can do

Complete the missing fields for your role:

| Role | Usually missing |
| --- | --- |
| Student | Program, major where required, year level, section, placement |
| Faculty | Academic and teaching details |
| Alumni | Degree and graduation details |
| Industry Partner | Organization name and primary program affiliation |

Cancelling onboarding removes only the requested role, and only while its profile
is still absent. Your other roles and your account survive.

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the **Secretary** if a field will not accept your information — for
example a program or major that does not exist in the catalog. The catalog is
their record; do not work around it by choosing something approximate.
