**Problem.** An evaluation you are trying to answer is closed and will not accept
answers.

## Check this first

- The evaluation's **Deadline**.
- Whether the person who published it closed it early.

## Why this can happen

- The **deadline passed**. Status is derived from the window.
- The publishing role **closed it explicitly**, for example because the response
  rate was sufficient.
- The **academic period was completed or cancelled**, which ends any evaluation
  whose deadline falls inside it.

:::caution
A closed evaluation cannot be reopened by you, and there is no late submission.
A draft that was never submitted is lost with the window.
:::

## What you can do

- If you need the evaluation reopened, ask the person who published it — a Faculty
  member for a Course evaluation, a Program Head for a Program-wide one.
- Anything you already submitted stays recorded; only unsubmitted drafts are lost.

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the publishing role. Only they can extend a deadline or close or reopen an
evaluation.
