**Problem.** You registered as an Alumnus or Alumna and your account has been
**Pending** for a while.

## Check this first

1. You are signing in with the **external** entrance.
2. Your **degree and graduation year** in the Alumni Profile match your
   institutional records.
3. You are not signed in as a different person — see
   [I signed into the wrong account](/troubleshooting/sign-in/account-match/).

## Why this can happen

- Verification compares your details against institutional records and is
  completed by the college, not by you.
- Small mismatches — a different program name, a graduation year off by one — are
  enough to hold the account.

:::caution
**Pending is not rejected.** It means nobody has finished checking yet. Your
account and everything in it is intact.
:::

## What you can do

- Correct any detail you know is wrong and save the profile again.
- Sign in periodically; pending accounts are released when verification completes.

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the college registry or your program office if it stays pending after the
usual verification window. If the account is **Rejected** rather than pending,
ask what detail could not be matched — a rejected account can be registered again
once it is corrected.
