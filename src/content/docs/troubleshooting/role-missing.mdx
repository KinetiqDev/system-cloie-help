**Problem.** A workspace or navigation you expect does not appear, or a role you
were told you have is not listed.

## Check this first

1. Are you looking at the **role selector**? Only assigned roles appear there.
2. Did you sign in with the same email address your account was created with?
3. If you were told you were approved recently, remember it may take effect on
   your next sign-in.

## Why this can happen

| Role | How it is granted |
| --- | --- |
| Secretary, Dean, Program Head, General Education Coordinator | **Pre-provisioned only.** Never self-assigned. |
| Faculty | Claimed at sign-in, then **approved by the Secretary** |
| Student | Claimed at sign-in; also created by the Secretary |
| Alumni, Industry Partner | Claimed at sign-in, then **verified** |

A role that is requested but not yet approved does not open a workspace.

## What you can do

- Sign out and sign in again — the active-role list is resolved on each session.
- Confirm the exact address on your account matches the one you sign in with.
- If you are registering a new Faculty account, remember the request needs
  Secretary approval: [Review Faculty requests](/secretary/faculty-requests/).

## When to contact an administrator

Contact the **Secretary** to have a role added. Roles are an account-level change
nobody else can make.
