As a Faculty member you own the course-level work: who is on the roster, what
the Course is meant to produce, and the evaluation that asks whether it did.

<VideoGuide title="Faculty onboarding" duration="5:00" />

## Your Course scope

:::caution
You see only the Courses where you hold an **active Course assignment**. Being
listed as affiliated with a program gives you no access to the rest of that
program's Courses.
:::

If a Course you teach is not on **My Course Rosters**, nobody has assigned you.
Ask your Program Head — or the Dean, or the General Education Coordinator for a
General Education Course.

## What you manage

- **Course rosters** — adding, importing, and removing Students in your assigned Courses.
- **CILOs** — authoring the Course's intended learning outcomes.
- **Outcome alignment** — mapping each CILO to a Graduate Outcome or an Institutional Learning Outcome.
- **Evaluation tools** — creating Course-bound tools from an available baseline.
- **Publishing** — publishing a Course evaluation to your own roster.
- **Reviewing** — reading submitted responses and Course-level evidence.

## What your Program affiliation means

:::caution
**Faculty Program affiliation ≠ access to every Course in that Program.**

Affiliation says where you belong academically. Teaching capability comes from an
active Course assignment, and only that. This is why a colleague can be in your
program and still have no roster of yours.

Your affiliation also tells System CLOIE which program a Program-wide evaluation
targeting Students may reach you through.
:::

## Typical Faculty workflow

1. [Check your Course rosters](/faculty/course-rosters/) at the start of the term and import the class list.
2. [Author the CILOs](/faculty/cilos/) for each Course you teach.
3. [Align each CILO](/faculty/outcome-alignment/) to a broader outcome. **Publication is blocked until every active CILO is aligned.**
4. [Create or derive an evaluation tool](/faculty/tools/) and bind questions to CILOs.
5. [Publish the Course evaluation](/faculty/publish-course-evaluation/) with a deadline.
6. [Review responses](/faculty/review-evaluation/) and read [Faculty analytics](/faculty/analytics/).
