**Analytics** is your read-only view of evidence for the Courses you are assigned
to.

## Choose a Course or evaluation

Use the filters to pick the academic term and then the Course or evaluation you
want. Only Courses where you hold an active assignment appear.

## CILO results

Each CILO's rating distribution comes from the questions bound to it in the
evaluation tool. If a CILO has no ratings, no evaluation bound a rating question
to it.

CILO results roll up to Graduate Outcomes through the alignment mapping and its
manifestation value. A CILO that was **Introduced** contributes differently from
one that was **Assessed**, and the figure reflects that.

## Written feedback

**Qualitative summary** and **Sentiment** describe the written answers that were
submitted. They report shape — how much feedback there was and its overall tone —
not what it means for your teaching.

## What the results mean

- Ratings are **respondent perception** collected through an agreed instrument.
- A high rating is evidence that respondents experienced the outcome; it is not a
  measurement of attainment.
- Results are comparable **only** between runs with comparable instruments and
  scales. Where a tool changed, System CLOIE flags the break.

## What the results do not mean

:::caution
- They are **not** a grade, and they do not feed into one.
- They are **not** a ranking of Students.
- They are **not** attributable to individual Students. You see anonymized
  responses only; the Program Head holds identified detail.
- They do not cover non-respondents. Low response counts make every figure thinner.

Always report the **completion rate** next to any figure you quote.
:::
