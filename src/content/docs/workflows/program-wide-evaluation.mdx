A Program-wide evaluation is not tied to a Course. It is targeted at a chosen
respondent group across one whole program.

## 1. The program foundation is in place

The **Secretary** has created the program, its Courses, and the accounts that
make respondents exist:

- **Students** with a complete profile and an active-term placement.
- **Alumni** and **Industry Partners** whose accounts are **verified**. A pending
  external account is not eligible for any deployment.

Without this, a Program-wide deployment reaches fewer people than expected — see
[My Alumni account is still pending](/troubleshooting/alumni-verification/).

## 2. The Program Head confirms the outcomes

The Program Head checks that the program's [Graduate Outcomes](/program-head/outcomes/)
are current. A Program-wide deployment carries a **GO binding**, so the evidence it
collects is evidence *about something specific*.

## 3. The Program Head builds the tool

Either [derived from an institutional baseline](/program-head/tools/) or built
blank. A Program-wide tool is not bound to Course CILOs — the GO binding is chosen
at publication instead.

## 4. The Program Head publishes the deployment

From the tool, choose **Continue to publish**, then:

1. **Choose the respondent group** — Students of the program, Alumni, or Industry Partners.
2. **Set the availability window** — activation and deadline.
3. **Review the deployment** — who is included, who is excluded, and why.
4. **Publish**.

## 5. Respondents answer

- **Students** find it on their [Evaluations](/student/evaluations/) page.
- **Alumni** and **Industry Partners** find it on theirs, if their account is
  verified and their program affiliation matches.

Each respondent gets one response. Drafts save section by section; submission is
final.

## 6. The Program Head reviews evidence

- [Review responses](/program-head/responses/) — completion, individual submitted
  responses, and qualitative feedback. Identified detail is Program-Head-only.
- [Program analytics](/program-head/analytics/) — GO evidence, stakeholder
  comparison, trends, qualitative summaries.

:::caution
Evidence sources are kept **separate, never pooled**. A Student figure and an
Alumni figure about the same GO describe different populations. Report them
separately, each with its completion rate.
:::

## Known limits

- Industry Partner **multi-program affiliation** is still an open policy — a partner
  affiliated with several programs can only carry one primary affiliation.
- **Program report export is a stub.** Use analytics and the response review
  instead: [Reports](/program-head/reports/).
- Small-group **privacy suppression** behaviour is still an open product decision —
  do not assume a specific rule.
