Turning a term over is a small number of decisions with long-lasting
consequences, because the readiness picture of the old term is kept.

## 1. The Secretary prepares the next period

Before anything moves, the next **school year, semester, or term** must exist and
have dates. Exactly one period is **active** at a time.

:::caution
Completing or cancelling a period **ends any evaluation whose deadline falls
inside it**. Tell Faculty before you close a term that still has open
evaluations.
:::

## 2. Rollover previews the move

The Secretary [previews the rollover](/secretary/term-rollover/) from the
**Term Rollover** workspace: a **Source Term**, a **Target Term**, the
**Students to Process**, the **Cohort Promotion Rules**, and any
**Exceptions Requiring Review**.

An exception is a Student whose record cannot be moved automatically. Read them
before confirming — the preview exists so nobody has to guess.

## 3. Rollover moves placements, and nothing else

Confirmed, rollover advances Student academic **placements** to the target term.
Program, major, year level, and section carry over as recorded.

It does **not** create Course assignments, move anyone between Course rosters,
touch accounts or roles, or delete any response or evaluation history.

## 4. Readiness is snapshotted at completion

Completing the period persists an immutable **readiness snapshot**. That snapshot
is what the Dean's historical views read, which is why last term's alignment state
stays accurate after the fact.

## 5. The Dean reads the closed term

[Learning-outcome readiness](/dean/learning-outcomes/) can be switched to the
completed period to see the alignment state as it stood at term end — archived
outcomes appear there labelled **(Archived)**. That comparison, term over term, is
the point of the snapshot.

## 6. Assignments and rosters are rebuilt for the new term

This is the part that is *not* automatic. For each Course:

1. A Program Head or the General Education Coordinator creates the assignment for
   the new period — [Program-specific Course assignments](/program-head/course-assignments/).
2. The Faculty member [builds the roster](/faculty/course-rosters/) again.
3. CILOs and their alignments carry over, since they belong to the Course.

## What graduating Students do at rollover

Nothing changes. Term rollover only moves placements. Converting a graduated
Student to **Alumni** is a separate, confirmed step — see
[Transition a graduated Student to Alumni](/secretary/student-to-alumni/) and
[How a Student becomes an Alumni](/workflows/student-to-alumni/).
