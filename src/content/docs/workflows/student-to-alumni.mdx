## 1. Term rollover identifies graduating Students

[Term rollover](/secretary/term-rollover/) reports who is in their graduating
cohort. It moves their placements and nothing else — nobody becomes an Alumni at
this point.

## 2. Final-term evaluations are completed

A graduating Student may see an extra evaluation their year did not — a
Program-wide graduating evaluation, or a final-term Course evaluation. See
[Evaluations for graduating Students](/student/graduating/).

Keep the term open until those evaluations close.

## 3. The institution confirms graduation

The Secretary's role. The College confirms the degree was completed; rollover
data is a signal, not an authority.

## 4. The Secretary performs the transition

On the account: remove the **Student** role, add the **Alumni** role, and complete
the **Alumni Profile** with the degree and graduation details that verification
will use. See
[Transition a graduated Student to Alumni](/secretary/student-to-alumni/).

:::caution
Removing the Student role removes **roster eligibility immediately**. Coordinate
with the Program Head before doing this mid-term, or the Student loses access to
evaluations they are still expected to answer.
:::

If the person is also still taking a course, keep both roles. Only one is active
at a time.

## 5. Historical Student evidence is preserved

Everything submitted as a Student is retained, unchanged and attributable to the
terms it was collected in:

- every evaluation they submitted and the answers they gave;
- their program, major, year level, and placement history;
- their Student submission history entries.

Nothing is deleted or rewritten. Deactivating or converting an account stops future
access; it never removes responses already collected.

## 6. The person signs in as an Alumni

They sign in with **Google or email and password** — Alumni is one of only two
roles allowed either — and select **Alumni** as the active role.

Their verification state:

- **Verified at creation** if the Secretary built the profile — immediate.
- **Pending** if they self-registered — the workspace opens only once institutional
  verification completes.

## 7. Future respondent work happens as an Alumni

[Alumni-targeted evaluations](/alumni/evaluations/) are published by Program Heads
against the program, reach accounts affiliated with that program, and appear
alongside the preserved Student record in
[submission history](/alumni/history/).

:::caution
**Graduating Student is not a ninth role.** There are eight. A graduating Student
is a Student with a graduation condition — nothing about the account type changes.
:::
