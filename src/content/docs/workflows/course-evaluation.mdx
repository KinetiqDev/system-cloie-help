One Course evaluation is the system at its most ordinary. Follow it once and the
rest of System CLOIE makes sense.

## 1. The Course assignment is prepared

A **Program Head** — or the **General Education Coordinator** for a General
Education Course — assigns a Faculty member to a Course, for one academic period,
year level, and section.

That assignment is the container. Nothing can exist inside it until it does.

## 2. Faculty prepares the roster and CILOs

The assigned Faculty member does two things in parallel:

- [Builds the roster](/faculty/course-rosters/) — adds Students manually or
  reconciles a class list by CSV.
- [Authors the CILOs](/faculty/cilos/) — what this Course is meant to produce.

A Student must be an active Student with a complete profile and an active-term
placement to be evaluation-eligible. Placement is institutional; the roster is the
teacher's.

## 3. Faculty checks outcome alignment

Each active CILO is [mapped to a broader outcome](/faculty/outcome-alignment/) —
a GO for a Program-specific Course, an ILO for a General Education Course — with a
manifestation value.

:::caution
This step is not optional. **Publication is blocked until every active CILO is
aligned.** It is the step that makes a submitted rating mean something.
:::

The Program Head and General Education Coordinator review the result; the Dean
sees it college-wide, read-only.

## 4. Faculty prepares and publishes the evaluation

The Faculty member builds a [Course-bound evaluation tool](/faculty/tools/) from
an institutional baseline, binds rating questions to CILOs, and saves it.

Then [publishes](/faculty/publish-course-evaluation/) against the assignment,
after reviewing the **Respondent Preview** and setting a deadline.

At publication the tool version freezes, one respondent assignment is created per
eligible roster member minus exclusions, and ordinary roster writes lock.

## 5. Students complete the evaluation

Students in the roster see the evaluation on their dashboard during the window.
They [answer it](/student/complete-evaluation/), [save a draft](/student/save-draft/)
if they need more than one sitting, and [submit](/student/submit/).

One evaluation accepts one response per person. Submitted means final.

## 6. Faculty and Program Head review evidence

- **Faculty** read [anonymized responses](/faculty/review-evaluation/) and Course-level evidence in [Faculty analytics](/faculty/analytics/). They never see who responded.
- **The Program Head** reads [identified responses](/program-head/responses/) and [Program analytics](/program-head/analytics/), where CILO ratings roll up to GOs through the mappings and manifestation values.

Each reports the completion rate alongside any figure. Evidence with a low
response count is thinner evidence, and the workflow expects that to be said
out loud.

## Where it can stall

| Stall | Owner | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No assignment exists | Program Head / Dean | [My Course assignment is missing](/troubleshooting/course-assignment-missing/) |
| Student ineligible | Secretary (placement) | [A Student is not showing on the roster](/troubleshooting/student-not-found-for-roster/) |
| CILOs unmapped | Faculty | [My CILO alignment is incomplete](/troubleshooting/missing-outcome-alignment/) |
| Publication refuses | Faculty / Program Head / Dean | [I cannot publish an evaluation](/troubleshooting/cannot-publish-evaluation/) |
| Respondents see nothing | Faculty | [I cannot see an evaluation](/troubleshooting/evaluation-not-visible/) |
