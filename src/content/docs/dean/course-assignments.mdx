As Dean you hold **all-program** Course-assignment authority: you can create,
reassign, and retire assignments for Program-specific and General Education
Courses alike. The Program Head and General Education Coordinator hold the same
authority only inside their own scope.

## What a Course assignment represents

One Faculty member, one Course, one academic period, with a year level and
section. It is the container that holds the roster, the CILOs, and the Course
evaluations.

## Create or edit an assignment

1. Open **Academic Structure → Course Assignments**.
2. Select **Assign Faculty to Course**.
3. Choose the **Course**. Course catalog defaults prefill the period, year level,
   and section — change them if this section differs.
4. Choose the **Faculty** member.
5. Complete the class assignment details and confirm.

:::caution
Defaults prefill the picker; they never rewrite history. An existing assignment
keeps the period, year level, and section it was created with.
:::

## Reassign Faculty

Edit the assignment and change the **Faculty** owner. Everything attached to the
assignment — roster, CILOs, alignments, published evaluations — stays with the
assignment, not with the person. The previous Faculty member immediately loses
access to that Course.

Reassign **before** the new term starts, not after a term has begun: a
mid-term reassignment removes the previous teacher's ability to finish the
evaluation they are running.

## Work with the roster

Open the assignment's roster from **Manage roster**. Eligibility for a roster
member requires an **active Student** with a complete profile and an
**active-term placement**. Anyone failing that shows as **Ineligible**.

The Dean can correct roster membership as part of assignment stewardship, but
roster maintenance during teaching belongs to the assigned Faculty member — see
[Course rosters](/faculty/course-rosters/).

## Deactivate or delete

Deactivate to retire an assignment while keeping its evaluation history. Deletion
is blocked while a published evaluation or a submitted response references it.
