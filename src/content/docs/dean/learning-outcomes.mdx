**College Oversight → Learning Outcomes** is a read-only, period-selectable view
of alignment across the whole college.

## What this page measures

Whether each Course-assignment context has a complete alignment structure: every
active CILO mapped to a broader outcome with a manifestation value.

It is a **structural readiness** measure, not an attainment measure. Ready means
"this evaluation could be published", not "the outcomes were met".

## Choose an academic period

Use the period selector to switch between the **active** period and **completed**
terms. Historical views are backed by the readiness snapshot persisted when the
period was completed, so they stay accurate after the fact.

Archived outcomes appear in **completed** periods, labelled **(Archived)**.

## Understand readiness totals

| Bucket | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Ready | Every active CILO satisfies the alignment rule |
| Missing CILO | The context has no active CILOs |
| Incomplete mapping | Some CILOs are aligned and some are not |

## Find mapping gaps and risks

The per-program rows rank alignment risk and let you open a context to see exactly
which CILOs are unmapped.

:::caution
General Education gaps are labelled **Institutional Outcome** gaps, never as
missing Program Graduate Outcomes. A GE Course supports an ILO; it is not
expected to support a program GO.
:::

Use [CILO Mapping Review](/program-head/outcome-alignment/) to see the same
mappings from the Program Head's side.

## Review historical periods

Select a completed term to see the alignment state as it stood at the end of that
term. This is how you compare a program between years.

## What the Dean cannot edit here

- Institutional Learning Outcomes — owned by the General Education Coordinator.
- Graduate Outcomes — owned by each Program Head.
- CILOs and CILO-to-outcome mappings — authored and maintained by Faculty.

You can review every mapping, and you can correct one under a documented
correction path, but normal maintenance belongs to Faculty. Say it that way when
you raise it with a program.
