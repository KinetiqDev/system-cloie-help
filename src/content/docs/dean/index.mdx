The College Dean holds college-wide oversight plus all-program authority over
academic structure, Course assignments, and institutional evaluation tools.

<VideoGuide title="College Dean onboarding" duration="5:00" />

## What you can oversee

- **Academic structure** across the whole college — programs, Courses, and their catalog details.
- **Every Course assignment**, Program-specific and General Education alike, including reassigning Faculty and working with rosters.
- **Learning-outcome readiness** across programs and terms, read-only.
- **Institutional evaluation tools**, including publishing on behalf of Faculty.
- **College dashboards** for the active academic period.

## What remains owned by other roles

:::caution
Two things a Dean commonly expects to edit, but cannot:

- **Outcomes and mappings.** You cannot edit Institutional Learning Outcomes, Graduate Outcomes, CILOs, or any CILO-to-outcome mapping. You review alignment readiness; the owning role maintains it.
- **User accounts.** Account creation, deactivation, and role assignment stay with the Secretary.
:::

Dean oversight also no longer includes class rosters as a separate oversight
surface — rosters are managed through the role-owned Course-assignment routes.

## Main Dean workflows

1. [Read the Dean dashboard](/dean/dashboard/) for the active period.
2. [Check learning-outcome readiness](/dean/learning-outcomes/) and follow a mapping-gap row to the program that owns it.
3. [Fix structural problems](/dean/academic-structure/) — programs, Courses, and their defaults.
4. [Correct Course assignments](/dean/course-assignments/) college-wide, including reassigning Faculty.
5. [Maintain institutional evaluation tools](/dean/evaluation-tools/) and publish on a Faculty member's behalf when a term is about to close.
