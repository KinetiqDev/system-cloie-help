**Academic Structure** is the Dean's view of the college's catalog. It groups
four pages under one navigation section:

| Page | Route | What you do here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Programs | `/dean/academic-structure/programs` | Review programs and majors; create or correct a program |
| Courses | `/dean/academic-structure/courses` | Review the Course catalog; create or correct a Course |
| Course Assignments | `/dean/academic-structure/course-assignments` | Create, reassign, and retire assignments college-wide |
| Evaluation Tools | `/dean/academic-structure/instruments` | Maintain institutional baselines and publish on behalf |

:::caution
Dean access to program and Course creation is **guarded**. Creation is limited to
cases where no other owner can act — for example a program that genuinely has no
Secretary available. Routine catalog upkeep stays with the Secretary.
:::

## Filter before you edit

Each page has **All Programs**, **All Scopes**, **All Semesters**, **All Statuses**,
and **All Year Levels** filters. Use them to find the record before changing it,
and confirm the **Course Scope** — it decides who may assign the Course and
which outcomes its CILOs align to.

## Deletion is deliberate

Deleting a program or a Course is **Deletion blocked** while anything depends on
it — assignments, rosters, CILOs, published evaluations. Prefer deactivation;
deleting leaves holes in historical evidence. See
[Manage programs](/secretary/programs/) and [Manage Courses](/secretary/courses/)
for the full rules.
