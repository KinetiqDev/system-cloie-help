As an Alumnus or Alumna you use System CLOIE to answer evaluations your program
asks of graduates — for example a graduate outcomes survey or a program review.

<VideoGuide title="Alumni onboarding" duration="3:00" />

## What Alumni use CLOIE for

- Completing **Alumni-targeted** evaluations your program has published.
- Checking what you have already submitted.
- Keeping your account and profile details current.

There is nothing else here. Alumni cannot see courses, rosters, other
respondents, or program records.

## Account entry

Alumni are one of only two roles that may sign in with **either Google or an
email and password**. Both routes end in the same workspace once your account is
verified.

See [Set up your Alumni account](/alumni/account/).

## Verification

:::caution
A self-registered Alumni account starts as **pending** and cannot open the
workspace until institutional verification finishes. A profile created by the
Secretary is verified at creation and skips this step.

Pending is not rejected. It means nobody has checked your details yet. See
[My Alumni account is still pending](/troubleshooting/alumni-verification/).
:::

## Normal workflow

1. [Check your profile](/alumni/account/) — keep your degree and contact details current.
2. [Open an available evaluation](/alumni/evaluations/).
3. Answer it. [Save a draft](/student/save-draft/) if you need more than one sitting.
4. [Review and submit](/student/submit/).
5. Check your [submission history](/alumni/history/).

## What you cannot do

- See who else responded, or any individual response.
- See your program's internal analytics.
- Edit a submitted response.
