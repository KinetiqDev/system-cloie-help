## Sign up or sign in

Choose the **Alumni** entrance from the portal, then sign in either with **Google**
or with an **email address and password**.

:::caution
Pick one method and stay with it. The external sign-in page accepts either, but an
account registered with a password will not accept Google sign-in, and an account
linked to Google will not accept a password. If you signed up with a password and
have forgotten it, use **Forgot password** — do not register again.
:::

## Enter Alumni information

Complete the **Alumni Profile** with:

- your full name;
- your email address;
- your degree/program and graduation year;
- your **Academic History and Verification** details, as the college needs them.

Accuracy matters here. These fields are what verification is performed against,
and they also decide which program may target you with an evaluation.

## Verification status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Account created; verification not yet completed. Workspace not yet open. |
| Approved | Verified. The Alumni workspace is open. |
| Rejected | Verification failed. See below. |

:::caution
While your account is pending, the Alumni workspace does not open — you sign in
successfully and land back at the pending notice. That is the verification gate
working, not a sign-in failure. See
[My Alumni account is still pending](/troubleshooting/alumni-verification/).
:::

If the Secretary created your profile for you, it is verified at creation and you
will not see the pending state.

## If your account is rejected

Rejection means your details could not be matched to institutional records. Check
that your degree and graduation year are correct, then contact the college
registry or your program office. A rejected external account does not block you
from registering again once the details are corrected.

## Keeping your details current

Update your profile whenever your email or name changes. A program targeting
Alumni by program affiliation can only reach you if those details are right.
