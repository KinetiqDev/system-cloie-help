An **Alumni evaluation** is published by a program to reach graduates like you. It
is not tied to a Course and not tied to a term you attended.

## Find the evaluation

Alumni evaluations appear on your **Evaluations** page once the program publishes
them and the window is open. Nothing needs to be requested or unlocked.

Check the **Available from** and **Deadline** dates on the card. Graduate
evaluations are usually published in a narrow window, so check the page rather
than waiting for a notification — the application does not email you.

## Answer questions

The mechanics are identical to a Student evaluation:

- **Likert** — rate a statement on the scale provided.
- **Guided Open-Ended** — answer a written prompt in your own words.
- **Predefined Responses** — choose from a fixed list.

Read **How to answer** first. The scale meaning is defined by the evaluation, not
by the numbers.

:::caution
Answer about your own experience after graduation. Alumni responses are compared
across cohorts, so answering as though you were still enrolled produces a figure
nobody can use.
:::

## Add written feedback

Written answers are the part programs most often cannot get elsewhere. Specific
examples — what a program did well, what it should change — are more useful than
general impressions.

## Save a draft

Your answers save section by section while the evaluation is in progress. A draft
is private and is not a submission. See
[Save an evaluation draft](/student/save-draft/).

## Review and submit

Read the review page in full, then **Submit**. Submission is final and one
evaluation accepts exactly one response from you. See
[Review and submit your evaluation](/student/submit/).

## After submission

- **Your response has been recorded.**
- The evaluation leaves your available list and appears in
  [submission history](/alumni/history/).
- Your name is not shown alongside your answers to the program.

## If nothing is available

Your account may still be pending, your program affiliation may not be recorded
yet, or nothing may simply be published right now. See
[My Alumni account is still pending](/troubleshooting/alumni-verification/).
