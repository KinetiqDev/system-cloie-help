A Student becomes an **Alumni** once they have actually graduated. The role
change is what lets them respond to Alumni-targeted evaluations later.

## When to use this workflow

- The Student has completed their degree and the registrar has confirmed it.
- Do not run it during term rollover. Rollover only moves placements.

## What history is preserved

Everything. A Student account that becomes an Alumni keeps:

- every evaluation they submitted as a Student, and the answers they gave;
- their Student program, major, year level, and placement history;
- their submission history entries.

Nothing is deleted or rewritten by the transition. Historical evidence stays
attributed the way it was collected.

## Perform the transition

1. Open **Users**, find the person, and select **Edit user**.
2. Remove the **Student** role from **Current roles**.
3. Use **Add a role** to add **Alumni**, and complete the **Alumni Profile** —
   the degree and graduation details used for verification.
4. Confirm the account is **Active**.

If the person should also keep a Student role — for example, they are still
taking a course — keep both. One account can hold both roles, and only one is
active at a time. See [Switch your active role](/start/switch-active-role/).

## What happens afterward

- The person signs in and selects **Alumni** as the active role.
- Their workspace changes to the Alumni dashboard.
- Course-bound Student evaluations they were eligible for disappear from their
  dashboard; previously submitted ones remain in
  [submission history](/alumni/history/).
- Alumni-targeted evaluations become available to them when published.

:::caution
Removing the Student role while the Student is still placed in an active term
removes their roster eligibility immediately. Coordinate with the Program Head
before doing this mid-term.
:::
