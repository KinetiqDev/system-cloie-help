The Secretary's **Course Assignments** page shows every assignment in the
college: which Faculty member holds which Course, in which period, year level,
and section, and whether the roster is populated.

## What this page shows

- The **Course**, its scope, and its program where Program-specific.
- The **assigned Faculty** and the assignment's **Academic Period**.
- **Year level** and **section**.
- Roster state and how many Students are **evaluation-eligible**.

Use the filter bar to narrow by program, scope, semester, status, year, and
section.

## Why this view is read-only

:::caution
The Secretary can see every Course assignment but cannot create, edit, reassign,
or delete one. This is a deliberate boundary, not a missing feature.
:::

Assignment authority sits with the role that owns the Course:

| Course scope | Who can change the assignment |
| --- | --- |
| General Education | **General Education Coordinator**, or the Dean |
| Program-specific | That program's **Program Head**, or the Dean |

If an assignment is wrong, ask the owning role to correct it, or raise it with
the Dean, who holds all-program assignment authority.

## Who manages assignments

- [General Education Course assignments](/gen-ed-coordinator/course-assignments/) — General Education Coordinator.
- [Program-specific Course assignments](/program-head/course-assignments/) — Program Head.
- [Course assignments across the college](/dean/course-assignments/) — Dean.

Faculty see their own assignments read-only on
[My Course Rosters](/faculty/course-rosters/).

## What is *not* in the Secretary's scope

Course **rosters** are maintained by whoever holds the assignment and, for
Faculty, by the assigned Faculty member on
[Course rosters](/faculty/course-rosters/). The Secretary does not edit rosters
here.
