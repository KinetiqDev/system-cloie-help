The Secretary is the institutional setup and record steward. You create the
people, the calendar, and the academic structure that every other role works
inside.

<VideoGuide title="Secretary onboarding" duration="5:00" />

## What you are responsible for

- **User accounts** — creating, editing, activating, deactivating, and approving Faculty requests.
- **The academic calendar** — school years, semesters, terms, the active period, and rollover.
- **Programs and majors.**
- **The Course catalog** — both General Education and Program-specific Courses.
- **Institutional evaluation tools** — the baselines other roles start from.
- **Viewing Course assignments** — read-only.

## What you do not manage

:::caution
Two things people expect the Secretary to own, but do not:

- **General Education Course assignments.** The Secretary sees them but cannot create, edit, or delete them. The General Education Coordinator owns those.
- **Institutional Learning Outcomes.** The Secretary has no ILO access at all; the Secretary's Learning Outcomes pages redirect to the dashboard. The General Education Coordinator owns them.
:::

The Secretary also cannot maintain CILOs, CILO-to-outcome mappings, or
Graduate Outcomes, and cannot edit a published evaluation.

## Recommended setup order

1. [Create the user accounts](/secretary/create-user/) — everyone downstream is attached to an account.
2. [Review Faculty requests](/secretary/faculty-requests/) — a Faculty role stays locked until you approve it.
3. [Create the school year and periods](/secretary/academic-calendar/) and set the active period. Nothing is evaluable without an active period.
4. [Create programs and majors](/secretary/programs/).
5. [Create Courses](/secretary/courses/) in the catalog.
6. [Create institutional evaluation tools](/secretary/evaluation-tools/) so Program Heads have a baseline to derive from.
