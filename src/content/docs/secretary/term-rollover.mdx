Term rollover moves Student academic placements from one term to the next. It is
a deliberate, previewable operation — run the preview first and read the
exceptions before you confirm.

## What rollover changes

- Each processed Student's academic **placement** moves to the target term.
- Their program, major, year level, and section carry over as recorded.

## What rollover does not change

- It does **not** create Course assignments or move anyone between Course rosters.
  Faculty and the Program Head maintain rosters separately, once the new term is active.
- It does **not** touch accounts, roles, or profiles.
- It does **not** delete submitted responses or evaluation history.
- It does **not** automatically convert anyone into an **Alumni**.

## Preview the rollover

1. Open the school year and choose the **Term Rollover** workspace.
2. Choose the **Source Term** and the **Target Term**.
3. Review the **Students to Process** count and the **Cohort Promotion Rules**.
4. Read **Exceptions Requiring Review**. An exception is a Student whose record
   cannot be moved automatically — a Student with no placement, or a Student
   whose data cannot be carried into the target term.
5. Resolve or acknowledge each exception. If the list reads **No exceptions**,
   every Student can move.

## Review exceptions

Do not confirm with unresolved exceptions you do not understand. Each exception
names the Student and the reason; fix the Student's record, or leave them out of
this rollover, and re-run the preview.

## Run the rollover

1. Select **Confirm Term Rollover**.
2. Wait for the run to finish. The workspace reports **Students Processed** and
   **Rollover Complete**.

Rollover is idempotent in intent: re-running it for a term that already moved
does not double-advance a Student. Confirm the preview before you commit.

## What happens to graduating Students

Graduating Students are **not** converted to Alumni by rollover. They stay
Students, and their final-term evaluations remain available while the term is
active. Converting them is a separate, confirmed step — see
[Transition a graduated Student to Alumni](/secretary/student-to-alumni/).
