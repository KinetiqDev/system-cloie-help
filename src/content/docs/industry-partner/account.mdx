## Sign up or sign in

Choose the **Industry Partner** entrance, then sign in with **Google** or with an
**email address and password**.

:::caution
Use one method consistently. An account created with a password does not accept
Google sign-in, and an account linked to Google does not accept a password. If
you signed up with a password and forgot it, use **Forgot password** rather than
registering again.
:::

## Enter organization information

Complete the **Organization and Verification** section:

| Field | Why System CLOIE needs it |
| --- | --- |
| **Full name** | Your identity, as your sign-in provider reports it |
| **Email address** | Your account identity |
| **Organization Name** | The company or partner organization you represent |
| **Primary program affiliation** | Determines which program may evaluate you |

## Program affiliation

Your **primary program affiliation** is the program whose Industry Partner
evaluations you are eligible for. It is the single field that decides whether an
evaluation reaches you.

:::caution
A partner organization may work with several programs. Only one affiliation can be
primary, and support for multiple simultaneous affiliations is still an open
policy question. If an evaluation you expect is missing, your affiliation is the
first thing to check — ask your contact at the college to confirm it rather than
looking for a workaround.
:::

## Verification

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Account created; verification not yet completed. Workspace not yet open. |
| Approved | Verified. The Industry Partner workspace is open. |
| Rejected | Verification failed. See below. |

:::caution
A pending account signs in successfully but does not open the workspace. That is
the verification gate, not a broken sign-in.
:::

A profile created by the Secretary is institution-verified at creation and skips
the pending state.

## If your account is rejected

Rejection means the organization details could not be confirmed. Verify the
**Organization Name** spelling against how the college records it, then contact
the college registry or the Program Head who requested the evaluation.
