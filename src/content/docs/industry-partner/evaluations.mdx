An **Industry Partner evaluation** is published by a program to gather your
organization's perspective. It is not tied to a Course.

## Find the evaluation

Industry Partner evaluations appear on your **Evaluations** page once a program
publishes them and the window is open. You do not request or unlock them.

If you were expecting an evaluation from a particular program and it is not
here, check your **primary program affiliation** on
[your profile](/industry-partner/account/) first — an evaluation is only offered to
accounts affiliated with the publishing program.

## Answer rating questions

Rate each statement on the scale provided. Read **How to answer** first; the scale
meaning is defined by the evaluation itself.

## Add written feedback

Written answers are usually the most valuable part of an industry evaluation, and
the part a program cannot obtain from anyone else. Specific, concrete observations
about the program's graduates and their readiness are what programs act on.

:::caution
Write about the program, not about individual people. Feedback is reviewed
anonymized, and comments naming graduates or students cannot be acted on safely.
:::

## Save a draft

Answers save section by section while the evaluation is in progress. A draft is
private and is not a submission. See
[Save an evaluation draft](/student/save-draft/).

## Review and submit

Read the review page, then **Submit**. One evaluation accepts exactly one response
from you, and submission is final. See
[Review and submit your evaluation](/student/submit/).

## After submission

- **Your response has been recorded.**
- The evaluation leaves your available list and appears in
  [submission history](/industry-partner/history/).
- The program sees aggregate results and written feedback — not your name beside
  your answers.

## If nothing is available

Either nothing is published right now, your account is still pending, or your
program affiliation does not match the publishing program. See
[My Industry Partner account is still pending](/troubleshooting/industry-verification/).
