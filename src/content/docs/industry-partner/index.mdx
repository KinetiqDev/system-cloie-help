As an Industry Partner you use System CLOIE to give feedback on your industry's
perspective of a program — typically an internship or industry evaluation
requested by a Program Head.

<VideoGuide title="Industry Partner onboarding" duration="3:00" />

## What Industry Partners use CLOIE for

- Completing **Industry Partner-targeted** evaluations a program has published.
- Keeping your organization and contact details current.
- Checking what you have already submitted.

## What access does not include

- No Course rosters, no CILOs, no learning outcomes.
- No view of other respondents or of any individual response.
- No view of your partner's internal data.

:::caution
There is **no access code**. Evaluations reach a verified partner account directly;
there is no code to enter, and asking someone for one usually means the request
did not come through the proper channel. See
[My Industry Partner account is still pending](/troubleshooting/industry-verification/).
:::

## Account entry and verification

Industry Partner is one of only two roles that may sign in with **either Google or
an email and password**.

A self-registered account starts **pending** until the institution verifies it. A
profile created by the Secretary is verified at creation.

See [Set up your Industry Partner account](/industry-partner/account/).

## Normal workflow

1. [Keep your organization details current](/industry-partner/account/).
2. [Open an available evaluation](/industry-partner/evaluations/).
3. Answer it. [Save a draft](/student/save-draft/) if you need more than one sitting.
4. [Review and submit](/student/submit/).
5. Check your [submission history](/industry-partner/history/).
