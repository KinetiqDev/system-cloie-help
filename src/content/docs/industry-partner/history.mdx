**Submission History** lists every Industry Partner evaluation you have submitted,
with the evaluation name, the date you submitted it, and the **Submitted** status.

Reopen a row to read what you submitted. Submitted responses are read-only —
submission is final, and one evaluation accepts one response from you.

## Drafts do not appear here

Only submitted responses are recorded. An evaluation you started and never
submitted is not in your history.

## What the program sees

The program reads aggregate results and your written feedback. It does not see
your name attached to individual answers.

## Keeping the record useful

If your organization details change — a new address, a new contact — update
[your profile](/industry-partner/account/). Future evaluations are matched on the
organization and program affiliation recorded there.
