This page exists so the gap is documented rather than silently discovered. It
does not describe a working workflow.

<AvailabilityNotice title="report export is not implemented">
  The Reports page lists three planned reports — a Course-bound CILO summary,
  stakeholder deployment completion, and a program outcome attainment digest —
  and states that they are prepared for later export integration. There is no
  export control and no generated PDF or spreadsheet.
</AvailabilityNotice>

## What is not decided yet

The following have to settle before reports can be built at all:

- rating scales and how CILO attainment is computed;
- how GO aggregation and weighting work;
- minimum-response privacy suppression;
- Dean drill-down and report structure;
- server-side generation rather than client-side assembly.

## What to use instead

- [Program analytics](/program-head/analytics/) for outcome evidence, trends, and qualitative summaries.
- [Review responses](/program-head/responses/) for completion and individual submitted responses.
- [Review learning-outcome readiness](/dean/learning-outcomes/) for college-wide alignment structure.

## Dean reports

:::caution
Dean Analytics and Dean Reports do not currently resolve to a working page. See
the [Dean guide](/dean/).
:::
