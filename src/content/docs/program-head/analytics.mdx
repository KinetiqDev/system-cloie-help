Program analytics turns submitted responses into **source-aware evidence**. It
never pools sources, and it never invents a figure the responses do not support.

## Choose the evidence scope

Use the analytics scope filters to select the academic term, the Course or
evaluation, and the evidence source. Filters are period-aware; a term with no
submitted responses says so rather than showing zero as if it meant "poor
performance".

## Understand outcome evidence

- **Direct GO results** come from GO-bound questions on Program-wide deployments.
- **GO mappings** carry Course-bound evidence: CILO ratings flow to a GO through
  the CILO's current mapping and its manifestation value.
- **Bound Questions** shows which questions fed a figure, so it is always
  traceable.

:::caution
Where the mapping used to interpret a figure has since changed, System CLOIE
discloses that. A GO number whose underlying mapping moved is not strictly
comparable to one from an earlier term.
:::

## Compare available stakeholder evidence

Completion by stakeholder shows how each respondent group participated — Students,
Alumni, and Industry Partners are counted **separately**.

"Evidence sources are kept separate" is deliberate. A Program-wide evaluation and
a Course evaluation may ask about the same GO with different instruments and
different respondents; their results describe different populations.

## Read qualitative summaries

**Qualitative summary** and **Sentiment** are deterministic descriptions of
submitted written answers. They report shape, not meaning.

## AI-assisted interpretation

**AI-generated insight** is a bounded supplement computed over an aggregate
packet:

- no raw comments cross the boundary;
- no respondent identifiers cross the boundary;
- nothing is persisted as a record of a respondent;
- it never replaces human CQI judgement.

Treat it as a prompt for your own reading, not as a finding.

## What analytics do not mean

- Analytics is **not** attainment. A high rating is evidence about respondent
  perception, not a measured learning gain.
- Analytics is **not** comparable across instruments. Where comparability breaks,
  System CLOIE says so.
- Analytics is **not** a ranking of Faculty or Students.
