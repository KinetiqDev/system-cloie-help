**Outcomes** holds your program's **Graduate Outcomes (GO)**. You are the only
role that can change them.

:::caution
The application says **Graduate Outcome (GO)**. Older material may say "PLO" or
"program learning outcome"; search for both, but write GO.
:::

## What a Graduate Outcome is

A program-level statement of what a graduate of your program should be able to
do. Each Course's CILOs are mapped to your GOs, and evaluations ask respondents
whether those Courses delivered them.

## Create a GO

1. Open **Outcomes** for your program.
2. Select the create action.
3. Enter the **GO Code** — short and stable.
4. Enter the description.
5. Save.

:::caution
You may create GOs for programs listed on your account **only**. There is no
"all programs" GO catalog.
:::

## Edit a GO

Open the GO and change its description. A GO code is referenced by mappings and
by published evaluations, so treat it as an identifier — renaming a code re-keys
the mappings that used it.

## Reorder GOs

**Drag rows to reorder.** Your order is the order used everywhere the catalog is
listed and in analytics.

## Archive and restore

Archiving keeps the GO readable in historical periods — it shows there labelled
**(Archived)** — while removing it from new mapping work. Restore brings it back
to active status.

Archive rather than delete. A deleted GO breaks the chain from an old evaluation
back to what it was measuring.

## How CILOs contribute to GOs

- **Faculty** author CILOs and map them to your GOs in the Course alignment workspace.
- **You** review the resulting picture at [CILO alignment review](/program-head/outcome-alignment/).
- **Evidence** flows CILO → GO through the mapping's manifestation value.

One CILO can contribute to more than one GO, and one GO can be supported by
CILOs from many Courses. Both directions are many-to-many, and System CLOIE
discloses that when it reports a figure.
