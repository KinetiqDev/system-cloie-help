You hold **Program-specific** assignment authority inside the programs listed on
your account.

## Your assignment scope

| Course scope | Your authority |
| --- | --- |
| Program-specific, in your program | Create, edit, reassign, deactivate, delete |
| General Education | **Read-only.** The General Education Coordinator owns these |
| Program-specific, another program | None. That program's Program Head or the Dean owns them |

## Create an assignment

1. Open **Course Assignments** for your program.
2. Select **Assign Faculty to Course**.
3. Choose the Program-specific **Course**.
4. Confirm the **Academic Period**, **Year Level**, and **Section**. Catalog
   defaults prefill them; they do not force them.
5. Choose the **Faculty** member and confirm.

:::caution
Only Faculty may be assigned. A Program Head cannot be rostered onto a Course they
do not teach.
:::

## Set period, year level, and section

These three values define which Cohort of Students the assignment serves. Set
them before the term starts: changing them afterwards does not move Students who
were already placed, and it does not move a running evaluation.

## Reassign Faculty

Edit the assignment and change the **Faculty** owner. The roster, CILOs,
alignments, and published evaluations stay with the assignment. The previous
Faculty member loses access immediately.

If a term has started and an evaluation is running, ask the outgoing Faculty
member to publish and close it first.

## Open a Course roster

Select **Manage roster** on an assignment to review membership and eligibility
without changing it. Roster maintenance during teaching belongs to the assigned
Faculty member — see [Course rosters](/faculty/course-rosters/).

## Deactivate, reactivate, or delete

- **Deactivate** retires the assignment while preserving its evaluation history.
- **Reactivate** returns it to service.
- **Delete** is blocked while a published evaluation or submitted response references it.

## Why General Education assignments are different

A General Education Course supports Institutional Learning Outcomes, not your
program's Graduate Outcomes, and one GE Course is usually taught by one Faculty
member across the whole college. That is why GE assignment authority sits with
the General Education Coordinator and is not delegated per program.
