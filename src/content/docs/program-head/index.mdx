As Program Head you are accountable for one or more programs: their Graduate
Outcomes, their Courses, their evaluation tools, and the evidence that comes back.

<VideoGuide title="Program Head onboarding" duration="5:00" />

## Your program scope

Your authority comes from the **managed programs** recorded on your account —
never from a URL or a remembered preference. If a program is not on your account,
no route will let you into it.

## If you manage more than one program

You pick which program you are working with at the start of each session. See
[Select the program you are working with](/program-head/select-program/).

## What you manage

- **Graduate Outcomes (GOs)** for your programs — the canonical current term.
- **Program-specific Course assignments**, including reassigning Faculty.
- **Program-owned evaluation tools**, derived from institutional baselines or built from scratch.
- **Program-wide deployments** to Students, Alumni, and Industry Partners.
- **Responses** — you are the only role that sees identified respondent detail.
- **Program analytics** across every evidence source your program has.

:::caution
Graduate Outcome (GO) is the current term. Older material may say PLO or
"program learning outcome"; they mean the same thing, but the application says GO.
:::

## What is read-only

| You can see | You cannot change |
| --- | --- |
| CILO-to-GO mappings across every Course in your program | **Faculty** author and maintain the mappings |
| CILOs on your Courses | Faculty author the CILOs |
| Institutional Learning Outcomes | General Education Coordinator owns them |
| General Education Course assignments | General Education Coordinator owns them |
| General Education Courses | General Education Coordinator assigns them |

## Typical Program Head workflow

1. [Select your program](/program-head/select-program/).
2. Confirm the [Graduate Outcomes](/program-head/outcomes/) are current.
3. Check [Program Course assignments](/program-head/course-assignments/) — an unassigned Course produces no evidence.
4. Review [CILO alignment](/program-head/outcome-alignment/) and ask Faculty to close gaps.
5. Maintain [Program evaluation tools](/program-head/tools/).
6. [Publish a Program-wide evaluation](/program-head/publish-program-evaluation/) for Alumni or Industry Partner respondents.
7. [Review responses](/program-head/responses/) and read [Program analytics](/program-head/analytics/).
