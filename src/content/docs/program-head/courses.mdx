The **Courses** page lists the Courses visible inside the program you currently
have selected.

## Program-specific Courses

These belong to your program. You can assign Faculty to them from
[Program Course assignments](/program-head/course-assignments/) and manage their
evaluation tools from [Program evaluation tools](/program-head/tools/).

Creating and deleting Courses in the catalog is **Secretary** work — see
[Manage Courses](/secretary/courses/).

## General Education Courses

General Education Courses also appear here so you can see the whole teaching
load your program carries, but they are **read-only** to you.

:::caution
You cannot assign Faculty to a General Education Course. The General Education
Coordinator owns every GE assignment, college-wide. See
[General Education Course assignments](/gen-ed-coordinator/course-assignments/).
:::

## What you can change

- Assignment-level settings for your program's Courses: academic period, year
  level, and section.
- The Faculty owner of a Program-specific Course assignment.

Course **catalog** details — code, title, scope, defaults — belong to the
Secretary.

## Filters

Use **All Programs**, **All Scopes**, **All Semesters**, **All Statuses**, and
**All Year Levels** to isolate what you need. Scope is the fastest way to tell
Program-specific and General Education Courses apart.
