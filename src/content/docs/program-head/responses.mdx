**Responses** is your review surface for both Course-bound and Program-wide
evaluations in your program.

## Choose an evaluation

The list is filtered by evaluation kind — **Course evaluation** or
**Program-wide evaluation** — plus status. Open one to see its detail view.

## Completion information

Each evaluation reports:

- **Assigned** — how many respondent assignments were created at publication.
- **Responses** — how many have been submitted.
- **Completion** — the ratio. It is a participation figure, not a satisfaction score.

In-progress drafts are never shown. Only submitted responses are reviewable, and
a draft belongs to its respondent alone.

## Response details

You are the **only role that sees identified respondent detail** — who responded,
in the context of your program. Faculty and the Dean see the anonymized flow
only. That boundary is deliberate and is enforced on the server.

Course-bound and Program-wide evidence are kept **separate**. Do not pool them
into a single figure.

## Qualitative feedback

Written answers appear per response. Their summary is a deterministic description
of what was written — it does not judge or rank respondents.

:::caution
Raw qualitative comments are **not** cleared for unrestricted reuse. Removing a
name does not make a comment publishable. Treat comment reuse as a separate
privacy decision, not as a reporting convenience.
:::

## Evidence and privacy

- Minimum-response suppression behaviour for small groups is still an open
  product decision. Do not build a practice that assumes a specific suppression
  rule.
- The AI-assisted interpretation available in analytics is bounded and
  non-persisted; no raw comment or respondent identifier crosses that boundary.

See [Who can see evaluation responses?](/concepts/privacy-and-response-visibility/).
