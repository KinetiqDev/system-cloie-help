The alignment review is your read-only view of how every CILO in your program
connects to your Graduate Outcomes.

## What the alignment shows

Rows are Course-assignment contexts in your program. Each row lists its active
CILOs and, for each one, the GO it is mapped to and the manifestation value on
that mapping. A CILO with no GO is shown as **Unanswered** — it is unmapped.

## Manifestation

Manifestation is how strongly a CILO delivers a GO:

| Value | Reading |
| --- | --- |
| Introduced | The GO appears on the Course, with light coverage |
| Reinforced | The GO is developed further on the Course |
| Assessed | The GO is evaluated directly on this Course |

Because it sits on the mapping, the same GO can be *Introduced* in one Course and
*Assessed* in another. That is intentional.

## Readiness and missing mappings

A context is **ready** when every active CILO has a GO and a manifestation value.
Unready contexts cannot have a Course evaluation published — that gate is what
keeps evidence traceable back to an outcome.

To close a gap, ask the assigned Faculty member to complete their alignment in
the Course alignment workspace. Unmapped CILOs are the most common reason a
program cannot publish.

:::caution
**Faculty maintain the mappings.** You review them, and you can correct one under
a documented correction path — but normal maintenance is not a Program Head
action. Say "you can review these mappings, but Faculty maintains them".
:::

## Reviewing an evaluation's CILOs

The CILO review pages go one level deeper: for a specific evaluation, what each
CILO scored, and how that flows to a GO. Use those when a figure surprises you.
