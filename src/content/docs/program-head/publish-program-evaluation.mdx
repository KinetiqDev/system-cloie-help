A **Program-wide evaluation** targets a chosen group of respondents across your
whole program, rather than the Students in one Course roster.

## Before you publish

- The program you are working in is the right one — see [Select the program you are working with](/program-head/select-program/).
- The evaluation tool exists and its questions are final. Publication freezes the version.
- The **GO binding** is chosen, so the responses become evidence for a specific Graduate Outcome.
- The respondents you want actually exist and are verified.

## Choose respondents

A Program-wide deployment targets a **respondent group**: Students of your
program, **Alumni**, or **Industry Partners**.

| Respondent group | Who appears |
| --- | --- |
| Students | Students placed in your program in the active academic term |
| Alumni | Verified Alumni accounts affiliated with your program |
| Industry Partners | Verified Industry Partner accounts affiliated with your program |

:::caution
Multi-program affiliation for Industry Partners is still an open policy. If a
partner should be included and is not, do not work around it by adding the
evaluation as a Course-bound one — raise it with the Secretary and the Program
Head of the partner's program.
:::

External respondents must be **verified**. A pending account is not eligible.

## Set the availability window

- **Activation** — when the evaluation becomes visible to respondents.
- **Deadline** — when it stops being available.

Both are optional at the tool level, but a deployment with neither window is open
indefinitely, which is rarely intended. Set a deadline.

## Review the deployment

The Respondent Preview lists who will be asked and who is excluded, with the
reason for each exclusion. Read it. Publishing creates one respondent assignment
per included person.

## Publish

Select **Publish Evaluation**.

After publishing, the deployment moves through **Scheduled → Active → Closed**,
derived from your activation and deadline. Changing the window is possible;
reverting a closed deployment is not.

## What happens next

- Respondents see the evaluation on their dashboard during the window.
- Each respondent may submit **once**. A submitted response cannot be edited.
- You read the results at [Review responses](/program-head/responses/) and
  [Program analytics](/program-head/analytics/).
