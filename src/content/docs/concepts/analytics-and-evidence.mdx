## What analytics summarizes

Only **submitted** responses. Drafts are never fetched and never counted. If no
one has responded, the page says so rather than showing zero.

Four canonical evidence sources are kept **separate, never pooled**:

| Source | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Student | Course-bound and Program-wide Student responses |
| Alumni | Alumni-targeted Program-wide responses |
| Industry Partner | Industry Partner-targeted Program-wide responses |
| Institutional | Evidence recorded directly against an outcome |

Program GO evidence flows through CILO question bindings and typed mappings.
Where the current mapping differs from the one used at publication, or where a
GO is supported many-to-many, System CLOIE discloses it next to the figure.

## What evidence is not

- **Not attainment.** A rating is respondent perception collected through an
  agreed instrument. It is not a measured learning gain.
- **Not a grade.** Nothing here enters a gradebook.
- **Not comparable across instruments.** Two runs are comparable only when their
  tools and scales are comparable. Where that breaks, System CLOIE says so.
- **Not complete.** Always report the completion rate beside the figure. A high
  score on 12 responses is thinner evidence than a middling one on 90.

## Human review

AI-assisted interpretation is a **bounded, non-persisted supplement** over
aggregate packets:

- no raw comments cross the boundary;
- no respondent identifiers cross the boundary;
- it never replaces human CQI judgement;
- nothing is stored as a record of a respondent.

Treat it as a prompt for your own reading.

:::caution
The qualitative summary and sentiment figures are **deterministic descriptions**
of what was written. They report shape and tone. They do not interpret meaning,
and a positive sentiment score is not evidence that a Course improved.
:::
