An **evaluation** is a tool version published against a target, with an
availability window and a set of respondents.

## Course-bound evaluation

Published against **one Course assignment**. Respondents are the Students on that
assignment's roster.

Published by:

- the **Faculty member** who holds the assignment;
- the **Program Head** or **Dean**, on their behalf. The Secretary may also
  deploy on behalf.

:::caution
Publication requires every active CILO to satisfy the alignment rule. See
[Outcome alignment](/concepts/outcome-alignment/).
:::

## Program-wide evaluation

Published against a **program** and a chosen **respondent group** — its Students,
Alumni, or Industry Partners.

Published by the **Program Head** within their authorized programs. A GO binding
is chosen at publication so responses become evidence for a specific Graduate
Outcome.

## Activation and deadline

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Activation** | When the evaluation becomes visible to respondents |
| **Deadline** | When it stops being available |

Both are optional, but an evaluation with neither is open indefinitely. During the
window, exclusions are reversible with **late inclusion**.

## Scheduled, Active, and Closed

Status is **derived** from the window, not set by hand:

| Status | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Published; activation has not arrived |
| **Active** | Inside the window |
| **Closed** | Past the deadline, or closed explicitly |

An `ARCHIVED` evaluation has no writer and can no longer change.

## What publishing fixes

- The **instrument version** is frozen.
- **One respondent assignment** is created per eligible person, minus recorded exclusions.
- **Ordinary roster writes lock** for that assignment.
- Course-bound snapshots record the CILO and GO bindings and the full assignment
  context at that moment.

This is what makes a response attributable later. Nothing about it can be
retrospectively adjusted to suit a result.
