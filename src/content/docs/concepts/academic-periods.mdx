Every date-sensitive thing in System CLOIE hangs off an academic period. If a
date filter looks wrong, or an evaluation will not open, check this first.

## School year

One academic year. It contains semesters, and possibly a summer.

## Semester and term

A **semester** is a block within the school year — for example First Semester.
A **term** is a period inside a semester with its own dates. Evaluation
availability, Course assignments, and Student placements all reference a term.

## Summer

Summer is its own period rather than a term inside a semester, and it may have no
terms at all. A Summer with no terms is normal, not an error.

## Active period

Exactly one period is **active** at a time. The active period is the default
scope for:

- Student placement and roster eligibility;
- the Dean's readiness dashboard;
- Course assignment defaults;
- evaluation activation and deadline interpretation.

## Why periods matter

| Question you are asking | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Why can't I be rostered? | Placement is checked against the active term |
| Why can't I see my evaluation? | Its window is expressed in the active period |
| Why is the Dean dashboard empty? | It is scoped to the active period |
| Where did this assignment go? | Assignments belong to one period |

Completing or cancelling a period closes any evaluation whose deadline falls
inside it, and persists a readiness snapshot that keeps historical Dean views
accurate.
