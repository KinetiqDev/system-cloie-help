Alignment is the connection that makes a rating mean something. Without it, a
response records an opinion about nothing in particular.

## Program-specific Courses

A Program-specific CILO maps to that program's **Graduate Outcomes**. Faculty
choose the GO and the manifestation value in the Course alignment workspace.

## General Education Courses

A General Education CILO maps to an **Institutional Learning Outcome**. Faculty
choose the ILO and the manifestation value.

:::caution
A GE context is never expected to support a Program GO. Where the Dean's
readiness view reports a gap in a General Education Course, it labels it an
**Institutional Outcome** gap — not a missing Program GO. That wording is
deliberate.
:::

## Manifestation

Manifestation is how strongly a CILO delivers an outcome, recorded on the
**mapping** rather than on the outcome:

| Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Introduced** | The outcome appears on the Course, with light coverage |
| **Reinforced** | The outcome is developed further on the Course |
| **Assessed** | The outcome is evaluated directly on this Course |

Because it lives on the mapping, the same GO can be *Introduced* in one Course and
*Assessed* in another — and the same CILO can map to several outcomes.

## Readiness

A Course context is **ready** when every **active** CILO has both an outcome and a
manifestation value.

:::caution
**Publication of a Course-bound evaluation is blocked until every active CILO
satisfies the alignment rule.** It is a hard gate, not a warning: a rating is
only evidence about an outcome if the mapping says which outcome it measured.
:::

## Who maintains it

**Faculty** maintain mappings. The Program Head and General Education Coordinator
review them; the Dean sees them college-wide, read-only. An unmapped CILO is
normally fixed by the assigned Faculty member completing their alignment.
