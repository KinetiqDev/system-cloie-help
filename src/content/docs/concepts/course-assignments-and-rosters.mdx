Three records that people use interchangeably, and one they confuse with all three.

## Course

The stable catalog record. A Course exists whether or not anyone is teaching it,
and it has no Students attached to it directly.

## Course assignment

One **Faculty member**, one **Course**, one **academic period**, with a **year
level** and **section**. It is the container for teaching work.

Assignment authority by scope:

| Role | Program-specific | General Education |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Secretary | read-only | read-only |
| General Education Coordinator | — | **full**, college-wide |
| Program Head | **full**, in their programs | read-only |
| Dean | **full**, all programs | **full** |
| Faculty | read-only, own assignments | read-only, own assignments |

## Course roster

The list of **Students** inside one assignment. It is the population a Course
evaluation goes to.

Roster management belongs to the assigned Faculty member. Roster members are
matched **by name** — there are no Student IDs in a roster.

## Student term placement is different

:::caution
**Placement is not roster.** A Student term placement is the institutional record
that a Student belongs to a program, major, year level, and section in a term. It
is set by the Secretary or by rollover.

Roster eligibility checks that placement: to be evaluation-eligible, a roster
member must be an active Student with a complete profile **and** an active-term
placement.

A Student can therefore be correctly placed in your program and still be ineligible
because their profile is incomplete.
:::

## Lifecycle

```
Course (catalog)
   └─ Course assignment (Faculty × term × section)
        ├─ Course roster (Students, maintained by Faculty)
        ├─ CILOs (authored by Faculty)
        ├─ CILO alignment (maintained by Faculty)
        └─ Published evaluation (frozen; locks ordinary roster writes)
```
